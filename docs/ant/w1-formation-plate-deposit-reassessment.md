## 2. Integrated judgment against the artistic intention

The Plate/Z approach already makes durable individual-time traces participate in a changing physical world. A new Plate can cover an old Plate; the older layer can remain exposed elsewhere, receive energy indirectly, and respond through altered coupling. Immutable birth history therefore does not imply a static present. Plate is an actual modeled vibration unit, not merely a picture, export or passive snapshot.

The missing link is more specific: **which event makes a new deposited layer, and what newly retained contribution or organization does that event assert?** Current production answers operationally with a field-qualified selected corridor plus scheduling. The W1 shadows tried to answer with activity episodes and their disappearance. Neither family identifies a separate, independently supported formation transition in these archives.

This is not a declaration that production has no material. Its births, material coefficients, layers and physical response are implemented modeling laws. It is a limit on interpreting their registration times and selected geometry as evidence of natural formation. A future artistic law may legitimately stipulate deposition; it must disclose what it assumes, what persists, and what consequence tests that assumption.

**Responsibility separation does not mandate continuous Material before Plate.** Formation and physical-layer birth may coincide in a Plate-native model. Provenance and compilation must still be distinguishable responsibilities so an episode, exporter or scheduler cannot silently become the formation law. Conversely, a continuous material state could precede layer compilation. Present evidence selects neither storage architecture.

## 5. Plate, Deposit and the present sounding world

[MaterialStratigraphy](../../src/lib/agents/ant/MaterialStratigraphy.js) stores immutable descriptors and appends each mask to 96×96 bottom-to-top per-cell columns. The newest occupying layer is locally exposed. Burial count is the number above that layer; burial depth sums **overlying** layer thickness. Descriptor age is reported separately. Missing thickness is inferred from mean half-width (`clamp(0.001 + 0.05*halfWidth, 0.001, 0.01)`); a field named meters is a model convention, not measured sediment height. A single Plate can be buried in one region and exposed in another. Passing time alone adds no overburden.

The present model also contains mutable state beyond these derived columns: SurfaceField, cumulative load counts, nodule qualification timers and born nodule descriptors, and the worklet's vibration/sleep/pulse states. Calling Deposit derived does **not** claim the whole world or every response can be reconstructed from masks alone.

The normal lab route injects [AntEnvironmentAudioBridge](../../src/lib/audio/AntEnvironmentAudioBridge.js), default `bornSurface`, into the controller. [SurfaceAudioBridge](../../src/lib/audio/SurfaceAudioBridge.js) compiles materials and routes feet to a single exposed top Plate using source-grid lookup. The legacy [AntAudioBridge](../../src/lib/audio/AntAudioBridge.js) is an alternate route, not this default architecture. Buried ANT nodules are not instances of the standalone generic SphereModel.

[compileSurfaceTopology](../../src/lib/audio/compileSurfaceTopology.js) builds a separate sparse 32×32 four-neighbor finite-difference layer per Plate from its mask, with diagonal edge bridging. Current spatial coefficients come from frozen descriptor material. Consecutive entries in each actual 96×96 local stack supply overlap-weighted same-XY Z springs; globally adjacent births need not be locally adjacent. The [surface worklet](../../static/worklets/surface-plate.worklet.js) applies reciprocal forces and evolving displacement/velocity states. Buried 12-mode nodules couple to an oldest/deepest valid foundation node; they are not ordinary direct-foot targets. Depth shapes nodule coupling, while the ordinary Plate solver does not turn stratigraphic thickness into a new stiffness law.

| Deposit interpretation | Present judgment | What an independent model would have to add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Derived local layered set | Sufficient for layer membership/order, exposure, thickness proxy and burial. No persistent Deposit ID needed | Nothing if it only names these relations |
| Spatial/dynamic composite | Existing Plate lattice, Z springs, nodules and evolving solver already model a coupled composite | A new law only for an explicitly missing response, not a second wrapper for existing springs |
| Independent material identity/state | No current evidence requires it; **Undecided / Research Gap** | An observable requiring retained bonding, slip, compaction, erosion, fracture or another declared state not derivable from complete present inputs/state |

Coupling explains indirect buried-layer excitation and response dependence on neighbors. It does not demonstrate bonded cohesion, mixing, material transport, erosion, compaction under load, or in-place rewriting of a Plate's character. Existing nodules already supply an authored secondary buried-structure mechanism; they are not evidence that a general Deposit ontology has been discovered.

Two overlapping sheets can be loose, slipping or bonded in possible physical models; equal masks do not decide which. Likewise two worlds with the same descriptor histories can have different oscillator states and therefore different immediate output **without** needing a Deposit variable. A proposed matched-state discriminator must include all relevant neighborhood, topology, solver, boundary and delayed-input state. Only after those are controlled could a separately observed response motivate an extra state. Current archives contain no such independent Deposit-response channel.

The original Z intention remains well served by durable layers whose present role changes through later history. Agents still move in 2D; derived depth and physical Z springs do not establish 3D locomotion or load-bearing sediment mechanics. Running controller ticks relax fields and advance birth/stratigraphy outside the agent loop, so zero-agent intervals are valid; this does not claim evolution while the controller is stopped. The stronger circulation intention—material conditions altering later formation/activity, transport or reorganization—is a future causal question. Storage persistence, exposed-only excitation and coupled vibration alone do not close that loop. Keep the 3 ms pulse, 75 ms recovery, ×50 damping and base default Off; mature realtime collapse stays a separate known limit.

## 6. Formation-derived Character versus conditions and consequences

The original [W1 character implementation](../../scripts/ant-w1-character.js) defines consolidation as the mean, over admitted cells, of observed chemical-and-mechanical qualification duty from first admission through closure. Its directional order is travel-weighted nematic order of unique actual same-cell-contact movement segments from run start through closure. Opposite directions share the same nematic axis. The selected segment set is deduplicated within an episode, not an exclusive credit ledger across episodes.

These are **formation-history summaries under a declared observer**, with different attribution intervals. They are not demonstrated constitutive properties: duty does not solve density/porosity/solidification, and contact-direction order does not establish mechanical anisotropy. Bounded values and complete coverage are useful diagnostics, not validation. Chemistry gates the archived support readout; qualification-bit co-occurrence does not identify independent chemical and mechanical contributions.

A character can eventually be intrinsic without requiring a new class or a particular number of axes. Its definition must state which retained change caused it and which controlled constitutive response it predicts. A memoryless response derived from existing fields is also a legitimate modeling possibility. A new state is required only if the chosen law or independently supported phenomenon requires it; “formation-derived” does not automatically mean “independent field.”

Keep three questions separate: **Character** describes the material property/state; **Environmental Condition** includes current exposure, surrounding layers, vibration and loading; **Organism Consequence** depends on those conditions and the organism's own state. The same character can have different consequences at different depths or loading regimes. It must not be assigned an intrinsically good/bad ant label or a sonic preset.

The signed displacement-controlled `p` witness in the [response design](w1-material-response-discrimination-design.md) remains optional independent mathematics. Its chosen histories and law guarantee the synthetic distinction if implemented correctly; that is a formal consistency check. ANT provides contact dose, ant-ID gait strength and modeled impulse excitation, not the witness's signed imposed deformation or conjugate force/work boundary. Repeating a fixed yielding displacement cycle can leave `p` unchanged while unsaturated SurfaceField receives new dose. Small-signal tangent can remain unchanged despite retained `p`. Neither formation, compaction, altered resonance nor ANT loading follows from a successful witness.

As limited external context, [Keim et al., *Memory formation in matter* (2019)](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/abstract/10.1103/RevModPhys.91.035002) discuss history encoded and accessed through system state and perturbation response. This supports treating operational memory/readout as a research question. The inference here—that persistence alone cannot choose ANT's formation/birth architecture—is our source-and-model assessment, not a material mechanism supplied by that paper.

## 8. Research dependencies and next-pass boundary

The defensible dependency is a set of gates, not a compulsory sequence of ontology classes:

1. Define one operational formation/deposition claim and the activity/environment inputs it uses. State its artistic/modeling status and the observation that could contradict it.
2. Jointly define the state/update and compatible response/loading boundary if a constitutive claim is made. Preserve a comparator using complete existing state. Do not select a constitutive law and later attach an incompatible contact proxy.
3. Attribute retained contribution/provenance. Compilation, episode partitioning and scheduler conventions must not silently supply that contribution.
4. Define Character only to the extent the same history/state predicts a material response. Formation and character may be one contract; two historical summary coordinates are not prerequisites.
5. Compile or create a physical Plate. In a Plate-native model this can coincide with formation, provided the previous responsibilities are explicit. Then test current exposure/burial/coupling effects; keep Deposit derived unless an aggregate phenomenon needs extra state.
6. Only after a material/condition claim is operational, test one organism consequence without feedback. Vitality, social influence, vibration climate and return feedback remain later, separately authorized links.

The [formation gate](w1-material-formation-design-gate.md) is retained. Its field-only null explains audited fields, not every possible world. The [response design](w1-material-response-discrimination-design.md) is retained as a standalone formal witness and loading warning, not the necessary next ANT experiment. Original Plate-native formation is reopened as a legitimate competing design, not automatically selected. No new character axes, Deposit identity, or W2+ link is required by this reassessment.
