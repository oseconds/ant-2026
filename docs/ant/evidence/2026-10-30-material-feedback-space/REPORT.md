## Working architecture and stop

**Keep now:** current10% locomotion feedback, native chemistry/movement/contact/formation, immutable material, post-commit geology, and read-only max-D/X research context.

**Strongest research extension:** material-conditioned chemical residence, independent of secretion and other treatments. Its proposed meaning is: “a materialized surface changes how long shared chemical traces remain there.” This is a local environmental condition, not a command to choose another route. The half-life bound15–30s and layer scale4 are authored screening conventions; nearest316-to96 lookup is technical discretization. None is inferred as a natural material constant.

The world model permits slower processes continuing without agents and feedback changing conditions rather than destinations. Residence is compatible with that design contract. Compatibility is not adoption evidence. Ordinary repeated layering remains allowed. Stronger feedback is not required to make ANT a persistent world.

The sequence stops under the user's “distinct promising responsibility” and “unresolved architectural meaning before further experiments” conditions. No additional mechanism is needed merely to escape entry bias. The smallest genuine artist/modeling choice is whether material should condition chemical residence at all, or remain mechanical/geological. The numerical bound is not delegated as an artistic parameter decision.
