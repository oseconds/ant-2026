## Preferred architecture direction

If designing from this world today, use a persistent material ledger feeding an explicit **spatial mechanical representation**, with a separately declared observation/readout model. Deposition identity must remain available for provenance, chronology, exposure and constitutive inputs. It should not, by interface contract alone, require one independently bounded acoustic layer and two new output pickups for every deposition episode.

Retain local finite contact, spatial vibration, retained material conditions and indirect coupling. The present packed solver is a useful reference implementation of these responsibilities. Replacing it with unrelated per-episode tones would discard precisely the local material causality the world provides. Conversely, the present two-node median and row-major pickup recipe are insufficient grounds for calling every local descriptor a meaningfully distinct resonant body.

This direction is an architecture/modeling proposal. The evidence does not yet specify a new constitutive law or demonstrate a perceptually better sound.

## Two serious physical interpretations

1. **Explicit stratified mechanical network.** Preserve separate lamina motion where material chronology creates a mechanical interface. Resolve small support geometries honestly, preserve local interlayer coupling, and place readout in world space independently of deposition bookkeeping. Persistent vibration belongs to resolved mechanical degrees of freedom. This is the conservative preferred reference because it retains already implemented material-history responsibilities. It need not preserve the current Plate descriptor contract, binary coarse raster or per-Plate pickups.
2. **Effective deposited surface/column network.** Retain exact material history outside audio, but reduce bonded columns into local effective mechanics and simulate one field per mechanically continuous surface/component. This can remove redundant motion state and express consolidation. It discards independent interlayer slip/motion unless separately represented. It requires a defensible bonding/effective-property law; simply unioning adjacent masks or summing coefficients would invent one.

The first is the preferred starting physical interpretation until coupling/contribution evidence supports compression. The second remains serious only if ANT intends deposits to bond into a consolidated material. Their difference is not decided by implementation convenience or by a global ERB cosine. The artist's meaning of deposited continuity is relevant.

## Minimal falsification scope

The current pass tests the existing model's granularity, identical coarse filters, registration sensitivity, continuous-history output and real workload. It will not implement a new bulk constitutive law merely to produce an alternative recording. A prototype that changes geometry resolution without scaling mechanics is a different model, not a convergence result. A union-component resonator without a joining law is not evidence for consolidation. A dormant modal substitute cannot be assumed equivalent to the current field.

The historical challenge will ask whether earlier proposals independently agree with this direction, reveal an omitted responsibility, or were already falsified. They receive no authority over this record.
