# Independent diagnosis before the historical challenge

This source-only record precedes new capture/render results and detailed inspection of historical alternative-audio proposals. A separate Luna Max reviewer inspected the current source without those proposals. The parent checked the compiler, bridge, controller ordering and recurrence independently. This is a provisional diagnosis, not the final architecture verdict.

## Responsibilities present in the code

The production reference is one shared stereo AudioWorklet, not a node per Plate. Each immutable material descriptor nevertheless supplies a separate masked solver layer. The worklet packs all layers into common node arrays. It propagates within each layer, exchanges vibration through local vertical overlap links, couples native Nodule modes to their foundations, and sums two designated pickups per layer. Contacts directly excite exposed material only; buried material can respond indirectly.

The compiler retains exact 96-grid masks for source-column topology and exposure routing. Vibration geometry is a binary any-occupied reduction to 32-grid cells. A coarse cell does not retain partial-occupancy mass or subcell geometry. Production edges can add diagonal bridging cells. Local research descriptors have no such edges. Four-neighbor acoustic connectivity must therefore be measured separately from eight-neighbor material connectivity.

The relevant Plate inputs are mask, edges, two pickups, material coefficients, ID and order. B/C labels, episode identity, centerline, age and most other provenance are not independent acoustic inputs. Their causal relevance may still survive indirectly through geometry, placement, overlap and frozen properties. Local descriptors currently share fixture-derived material values and row-major endpoint pickups; those are research compatibility choices, not an established local-material law. A negative individuality result under this fixture cannot establish that material history has no meaningful character.

The bridge posts hardness metadata, but the worklet's strike recurrence does not read hardness. Contact position/strength/footprint and exposed layer determine excitation. A node retains vibration, a finite 18-step contact-force ring, 75 ms compliance memory, sleep state and compatible state across topology installs. Turning Audio Off clears dynamic vibration; retained material survives. This pass will distinguish material persistence from continuous vibration persistence.

Plate thickness and current depth do not directly set the Plate recurrence's mass or bending coefficient. They can affect Nodule geometry/burial. Each Nodule has twelve modes. Consequently a zero-Nodule replacement world changes both the planar vibration regime and this additional resonator/coupling path.

## Cost hypothesis from source

Continuous cost should follow awake nodes, stencil work, active local Z links, contacts and Nodule modes, rather than descriptor count alone. A contact scans its target layer's nodes to normalize and apply its Gaussian. Each material revision recompiles all descriptors/source columns, transfers topology and allocates/copies compatible state. Descriptor count and revision frequency can therefore matter mainly through topology work even when the continuous solver is small.

The fixed four-neighbor stencil imposes boundary restoring behavior even on a one-node layer. Many tiny deposits could become repeated copies of one elementary filter, with identical left/right pickups. This is a testable acoustic-granularity concern, not yet a measured result. Different 96-grid patches can share a 32-grid geometry while retaining different exposure/overlap/contact histories; a geometry collision alone does not prove redundant full coupled responses.

## Initial serious options and falsifiers

1. **Retain the coupled spatial-layer reference.** It preserves distinct lamina motion and local overlap coupling. This remains coherent if discretized local geometry, contact response, indirect buried contribution and measured web cost justify those degrees of freedom. Test continuous natural histories and the actual node/connectivity distribution first.
2. **Keep a spatial vibrating world, but let acoustic discretization and constitutive continuity define state rather than deposition labels.** Material IDs/history remain in stratigraphy. Any compression into effective columns or joining of adjacent deposits needs an explicit mechanical assumption; a union component is not automatically a physical body. Test the smallest concrete compression/readout change against information it intentionally removes before recommending it.

No oscillator bank, global score, preset timbre family, or independent resonator for every provenance label follows from the world model. Conversely, broad spectral similarity cannot justify discarding distinct material motion. The initial preference is to preserve local contact, geometry and retained coupling while testing whether deposition-level boundaries and pickups still express meaningful distinctions. The experiment may retain or reject that preference.

## Decisive measurements still needed

- Solver nodes and connected subgraphs per layer; one-node prevalence; identical absolute and translation-independent coarse geometry; pickup coincidence; coefficients; source occupancy versus coarse occupancy.
- Continuous fixed-window output with exact model-time replay, finite state and native topology-copy conformance.
- Direct strikes versus indirect state/readout contribution, including layers never directly struck.
- Runtime attribution with observer overhead kept separate from clean cost.
- A minimal falsification of any proposed compression, spatial joining, or readout change. Neither a controlled-rest render nor a shape-only ERB comparison establishes natural sonic equivalence.

Historical alternatives will be considered only after this diagnosis and measured candidate selection are recorded.
