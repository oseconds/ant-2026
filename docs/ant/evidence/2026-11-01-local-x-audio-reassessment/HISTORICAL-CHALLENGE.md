# Historical challenge: deposition identity and acoustic body

**Scope:** late challenge to the frozen diagnosis and candidates in [INDEPENDENT-DIAGNOSIS.md](INDEPENDENT-DIAGNOSIS.md) and [CANDIDATES-BEFORE-HISTORY.md](CANDIDATES-BEFORE-HISTORY.md). No source, simulation, audio render, or new seed was added here. Archived text below is historical design rationale, not implementation authority.

## What the historical proposal says

“Deposition unit ≠ acoustic body unit” is a synthesis of the older proposal, not its verbatim rule. The 2026-10-11 terminology note defines a Plate as a historically born layer that is also a physical/vibrating unit in the then-current model. Its provisional “Deposit” is an aggregate name, not a tracked identity or response law; overlap alone does not establish a coherent body. It explicitly leaves open whether a composite body exists beyond per-Plate motion and coupling ([archived boundary note](../../archive/2026-10-11-plate-deposit-material-boundary.md)). The linked earlier concept distinguishes an immutable birth record from present exposure, burial, coupling, and vibration, while identifying cross-time formation as unresolved ([archived sedimentary-circulation note](../../archive/2026-10-11-individual-time-and-sedimentary-circulation.md)).

That history agrees with separating **provenance identity** from an acoustic interface contract. It does not endorse treating Plates as passive records or replacing their modeled response with one aggregate body.

The later [archived independent-review synthesis](../../archive/2026-10-26-independent-review-argon-high-astra-high.md) was also checked after candidate selection. It distinguishes an intentionally different burial/metamorphosis model from lossless dormant-ROM substitution, and records retraction of unmeasured realtime guarantees. Its selective-listening proposal changes observation rather than solver work. Those are useful challenges to the present responsibility split, not evidence that consolidation, modal replacement or a new readout succeeds. The user-supplied deposition/acoustic-separation hypothesis is evaluated here as a proposition; this package does not attribute its exact wording or a validated law to an older document.

These are reasons to question ID-bound mechanics, not permission to union masks or discard state. |
| Did the history account for the observation model? | **Only partly.** It connects material and sound conceptually, but does not choose world-space readout, explain how spatial observation preserves contact response, or specify how vibration state survives topology changes. | Is a separate persistent Deposit class needed? | **Not on this evidence.** The older note deliberately leaves the aggregate as terminology; current [replacement](../2026-11-01-local-x-material-replacement/REPORT.md) and [singleton materialization](../2026-11-01-singleton-real-stratigraphy-probe/REPORT.md) reports demonstrate retained layer, exposure, burial, overlap, and foundation relations without proving a new composite ontology. |

## Older acoustic evidence that constrains the claim

- **Selected Plate identity has bounded positive evidence.** The completed 2026-10-13 blind session records one listener judging all six selected natural p50 pairs different and the hidden A/A pair no-difference ([evidence summary](../2026-10-13-plate-sonic-individuality/README.md), [session export](../../subjective-listening/2026-10-13-plate-sonic-individuality-subjective-listening.json)). This supports distinguishability for those selected clips, geometry/contact conditions, and this listener. It does not establish population repeatability, recognition of deposition history as such, general live-world audibility, or a need for per-ID resonators.
- **Broad material-band separation was negative in one frozen recipe.** Across 36 matched native/observed-median pairs, the 2026-10-14 ERB analysis reports median full ERB-time shape cosine 0.999601, integrated 30-band cosine 0.999992, late-tail cosine 0.999994, and median fixed-gain RMS change +0.0027 dB ([report](../2026-10-14-plate-erb-material-contribution/README.md)). It is a negative result for a broad repeatable ERB signature under that isolated render recipe, with no human listening. It does not establish that formation history has no material character, and does not answer whether distinct spatial states should be coupled or consolidated.

## Preserve the dormant-ROM negative as tested

The 2026-10-07 dormant-Plate study rejected its **fixed-operator modal reduced-order path**, not spatial mechanics or a different consolidation model ([report](../2026-10-07-dormant-plate-rom/README.md)). Its exact failure was that a rank low enough to approach arithmetic break-even did not preserve the tested response/state: rank 8 was about 0.97× full estimated work for a natural Plate 29 trace but had 50.2% output RMS error and 48.4% state error. Rank 16 cost about 1.60× full; rank 64 about 5.38× with 2.00% output and 3.19% state error; rank 128 cost about 10.42× full. Weak-event accuracy also needed ranks beyond break-even, and safe handoff checks added work. The harness was open-loop on a fixed topology; it did not validate reciprocal feedback, topology changes while dormant, direct-contact wake, long-run drift, or browser deadline performance. **Do not repackage this rejected route as a new solution or use it as proof that effective-column compression fails.**

The 2026-10-06 natural runtime recheck separately documents real-time collapse: at 30 historical Plates / 32 Nodules, all 9,806 solver nodes were active, no nodes slept, and late audio/wall ratios were 0.1258–0.1304 despite an `AudioContext` that remained `running` ([runtime report](../2026-10-06-runtime-recheck/README.md)). This establishes serious workload pressure for that historical build and route. It does not attribute the cost solely to deposition IDs, establish that local-X would improve it, or show that buried vibration is inaudible. The current local-X material studies were Audio Off, so they do not close that gap.

## Challenge verdict and artistic choice

The older record **agrees** with keeping material history and mechanical identity separable, and **misses** the concrete mismatch among source masks, coarse solver geometry, connected subgraphs, and pickups found in the present source diagnosis. It also makes a counterweight explicit: the Plate/Z history was intended to carry real physical and audible presence, not function only as an archive. No historical proposal decides whether neighboring or overlapping laminae should slip and resonate separately or behave as a bonded continuous surface.

The artistic decision remains whether sedimentary history should be heard as distinct layered motion, or whether formed deposits should merge into a continuous sounding body. The first favors independently resolved laminae where interfaces matter; the second needs an explicit bonding/constitutive rule. Evidence so far favors decoupling provenance IDs from the number of acoustic bodies as a **design question**, but does not prove compression, perceptual equivalence, or a replacement topology. The current preferred spatial-mechanics direction remains a hypothesis; a concrete prototype and a bounded information-loss test are required before recommending effective-column adoption.
