## Stage 2: revised priorities

### 1. Conditional transmission from a previously verified changed choice

**Still first, but the sampling question must change explicitly.** This pass tested an ordinary prospectively selected opportunity; it did not select a state with a known changed directional choice. Consequently it could not determine what happens *conditional on that choice difference*. It provides no reason to skip that missing link or add a mechanism.

Smallest next pass: before running nature, pin one record from the existing 284 wide-choice changes using a deterministic rule such as earliest timestamp then numeric ID. Recover its complete current-G world state once, and replay joint local/wide F/Fhat/Rhat through the same motor gate. This is an explicitly **effect-conditioned** study of transmission, not an effect-rate estimate or an unbiased natural sample. Do not scan its outcomes for a better case. A joint intervention may cancel the prior wide-only choice difference; that is an informative result, not a reason to substitute a case.

**Falsifies:** that a known wide-only changed choice necessarily survives joint sensing and actual motor constraints. If it does survive, the bounded continuation can test new inscription before shared-RNG schedule divergence. **Stop:** one pinned case, no changed joint direction/motor or failed sham/domain/RNG checks, otherwise the already specified short inscription boundary. A native post-tick clock-effect question would be a different estimand and must not be silently substituted. Research-only recovery/shadow is sufficient; no new production law.

This is narrower and more informative than repeating another arbitrary natural opportunity or expanding seeds until a visible divergence appears. This pass's result remains negative regardless of that future result.
