# Retained-material identity: contact-readout prerequisite

**Decision: MEASURE A PREREQUISITE BEFORE RENDERING.** Technical measurement complete, 2026-10-27 (Asia/Seoul). No new renderer or artistic result.

## Current problem and decision

The precise question is now whether material's **use history** can furnish a readable relation distinct from the current chemical trace. Adding several phenotype dimensions would not answer that question: it would change event provenance, spatial structure, temporal attention and the interpretation of simulation state together.

The proposed A/B/C is not a clean three-condition experiment:

| Proposal | What it could test | Current limitation / decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — fossilized exposed foot contacts | Actual event-linked accumulation rather than a decorated Plate footprint | A distinct hypothesis. It adds individual-event rendering memory; aggregate compaction/contact counters cannot reconstruct that archive. Eligibility, layer ownership and display containment must be explicit before glyph calibration. |
| B — world-locked medium | Shared spatial organization rather than trail-local styling | Existing C already has fixed canvas-pixel Bayer phase. At fixed 640×640 and camera, B as described duplicates its coordinate locking. A larger-scale or anisotropic spatial law could be different, but that would be a new specified manipulation. |
| C — existing View 4 Bayer | Reference the approach the artist found comparatively natural | Preserve as a practical reference, not an artistic winner or a control isolating only A's event provenance. A-versus-C changes visual grammar and temporal accumulation together. |

**Selected first pass:** resolve the exact event source, burial consequences and physical/display registration in the existing short H1 world. Do not render a new style before these choices are visible. A broad phenotype-range survey, acoustic redesign and Nodule presentation are unnecessary for this decision.

A fresh independent design critic preferred collecting this census inside an A-versus-C capture. The parent chose the smaller, measurement-only pass: an integrated capture would already have to decide mark placement, clipping and burial. The counterargument is retained in [the plan](PLAN.md). This measurement does not answer the perceptual question.

## Grounding: fact, evidence, judgement, hypothesis

**Production implementation facts, fresh main:** `aeefa12d8de9da400cb8a2a3d4b5080a21ba0292`. Production source and `docs/ant/README.md` were inspected. Plate birth is an authored discrete qualification. `MaterialStratigraphy` preserves occupancy/overlap/exposure/depth and aggregate valid-foot load; its current state is not a per-contact, event-time exposed-Plate archive. Ordinary direct strikes select exposed top material, while buried layers can participate through coupling. `SurfaceField` compaction relaxes on its own slow timescale. Layer count affects production locomotion; it is not literal rendered height. Birth-frozen geometry/material/pickups do not automatically provide live vibration visualization.

**Research implementation fact:** at research ref `da70d82822fa4d424744eaf6d50c53a728029ace`, `materialConditionedPheromone.js` indexes an 8×8 native-pixel-period Bayer screen at fixed canvas origin. It has no trail-relative phase, temporal randomness or RNG use. This is canvas-pixel locking, not a scale-invariant world-unit coordinate system. Source references are [listed below](#source-anchors).

**Experimental results:** the prior contour gate covered 97.75% of the boundary at +30 s and was near-full after birth. That invalidated the intended local selection distinction; it did not disprove contour as a family. Earlier edge/texture/relief comparisons establish particular applied manipulations, not universal laws about material perception.

**Artist judgement:** the recorded identity, smooth-relief and Bayer comments are preserved byte-for-byte in `previous-reviews/`. The Bayer comment says the approach is promising/non-alien but reads as some trails being different, not an area. The latest contour package still records artist review pending. The supplied request's broader chronology is useful author context; it is not silently promoted into a recorded contour judgement.

**Hypotheses:** longitudinal alignment may encourage line identity; shared spatial rules may or may not encourage a medium interpretation; use-linked accumulation may or may not feel different from decorative texture. Neither this measurement nor the previous artist comments establish universal perceptual rules. Chemical trace also has memory; current activity is not identical to chemical presence.

At +30 s, **130/816 (15.93%)** earlier routed contacts belong to a now-buried surface. Leaving every mark visible by world coordinate would introduce a readout of buried history; it would not be a neutral display of current exposure. Burial does not mean the physical material is dead or acoustically disconnected.

Physical support uses `round(x*95)` and `round(y*95)`. The canonical research display mask uses `floor((pixel+0.5)*96/640)`. Their disagreement is real under this coordinate convention: 304/816 eligible centres use different physical/display cell indices, although most still resolve to the same displayed owner. It does not establish a bug in the physical law or production's smoothed faint rendering. The live app is responsive and may use another device pixel density; the fixed 640 mapping is this research convention, not a universal production screen map. A future exact-coordinate glyph can be clipped/hidden at displayed support; relocating it to a cell centre would instead change the displayed contact position. Both are authored choices and must be declared. This pass repairs neither map.

The 803 point bins are **not** glyph coverage: no mark size, alpha, blending, ant readability or saturation was measured. The scalar-alpha counts apply `pheromoneValueToAlpha(field.sample(x,y))` at historical positions. The actual renderer maps field nodes, then resamples/composites them; these operations are not interchangeable. Therefore 144 is an opportunity proxy, **not** 144 visibly empty places and not 144 perceptually recoverable histories. It is not an aesthetic threshold.

## Decision after measurement

**Next priority: a small, Plate-owned contact-history shadow with a matched non-causal accumulation control.** Keep existing Bayer only as a contextual reference. The central question should be whether the relationship between actual contact and later persistent mark matters, rather than whether another texture is more attractive.

Before that future capture, fix these semantics explicitly: audio-independent eligible exposed contacts, event-time Plate ID, current-owner visibility under burial, exact-position versus clipped raster support, and one glyph/accumulation rule. A control using the same primitives and comparable accumulation can challenge the null that the result is merely added texture. This pass does not implement that control, define aesthetic success thresholds or select a final renderer.

The evidence justifies making these controls explicit: eligibility is independent of the transport toggle, while posting is not; early burial and display-map disagreement are already present. It does **not** show that fossils will read as material. A direct A-versus-C comparison alone would remain a comparison of whole visual/temporal grammars.

Material Phenotype remains a possible later architecture. Supported contact, compaction, overlap and Nodule qualification are not established independent axes. Transient contact-triggered lines would not automatically be measured resonance: genuine vibration-linked geometry would require a separate solver/readout contract. Persistence and long-run acoustic runtime remain separate questions; neither becomes a prerequisite for this no-DSP early visual investigation.
