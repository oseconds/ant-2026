# Recruitment and founder source audit

## Scope and decision

This is a read-only source/evidence audit on `origin/main` commit `8c60e4bba3bd5b476300e90a2936bfebd1f831c2`. No simulation, browser run, test, production edit, or recruitment-family experiment was performed here. `docs/ant/archive/**` was not accessed.

The current source has a **repeated fixed heading bias**, with modest position jitter around a fixed entry region. Heading is the stronger proximal founder-direction mechanism: every accepted generation receives a heading centered at `−π/2` with only `±0.3` radians of spread, while positions are sampled over a disk of radius `0.018`. Stage A supplies matched-seed evidence that changing the heading law changes the later live spatial distribution while leaving positions and spawn timing rules alone. The shared spawn point and material source selector remain separate constraints on the final material envelope.

The matched static heading screen supports heading as a causal contributor to later live spatial composition under the tested shared-stream policy. The independent contribution of spawn position, and whether heading alone explains a persistent material envelope, remain unresolved. The Oct 7 founder-effect package concerns low-evidence trail clocks/deposition, says recruitment was unchanged, and its controlled probes explicitly use one ant with no recruitment. It cannot establish the effect of spawn heading. It also predates the separately adopted retained-material locomotion resistance; do not reuse its old runs as a current-production recruitment baseline or revive any `resistance:false` variant as current behavior. Current production defaults to material resistance enabled (`AntController.js`, constructor; `docs/ant/README.md:30`).

## Implemented source facts

- `AntController` fixes `SPAWN_CENTER = (0.5, 0.72)`, `SPAWN_RADIUS = 0.018`, `SPAWN_HEADING = −π/2`, and `SPAWN_HEADING_SPREAD = 0.3` (`src/lib/agents/ant/AntController.js:40-48`). The point is not exact: each attempted position uses `angle = U·2π` and `distance = sqrt(U)·0.018`, which samples area uniformly in the disk (`AntController.js:451-455`). Position clearance is checked against live agents before an agent is accepted (`AntController.js:456-458`).
- `init()` creates the field and immediately calls `spawnNextAnt()` (`AntController.js:97-104`). Thus the first spawn is part of initialization, before any world movement or field evidence. A static control must cover that first agent as well as later recruits.
- An accepted spawn draws its heading from the same shared random function after position acceptance, passes that heading and the same random function into `AntAgent`, then schedules the next spawn (`AntController.js:451-464, 471-474`). Successful-spawn intervals are `400–900 ms`; a blocked disk retries after `120 ms`; placement gets at most 12 attempts (`AntController.js:44-48, 451-474`).
- Recruitment count is a separate mechanism. `ColonyActivation` starts at its floor of 8 and responds up to its configured ceiling through the current population law; `syncRecruitment()` schedules gradual replacement/recruitment and never culls living agents when desired population falls (`AntController.js:477-483`, `src/lib/agents/ant/ColonyActivation.js:36-47`). Keep this law and cadence fixed in heading controls.
- `AntAgent` starts with zero velocity, sets both `heading` and `intentHeading` from the supplied heading, and draws the same seven personality values and behavior clocks from the injected random function (`src/lib/agents/ant/AntAgent.js:7-32`). It has no social role or neighbor memory.
- `ColonyActivation` samples 16 angular sectors at radii `0.06`, `0.09`, and `0.12` around the same origin. It saturates each sample, sorts sector values, and averages only the four largest (`src/lib/agents/ant/ColonyActivation.js:1-7, 57-73`). Sorting discards sector identity: the current result drives a scalar population response and does not provide a recruitment direction. The selected Stage B shadow computes a circular resultant from the same pheromone-sector samples as a new directional use of field occupancy; it is not existing directional recruitment.
- `PheromoneField` is a scalar 96×96 field with bilinear samples and 15 s default half-life. Deposition records traveled distance through a spatial brush; concentration records where material was laid, not whether ants traveled outward or inward (`src/lib/agents/ant/PheromoneField.js:1-4, 13-38, 43-77`). Pheromone-sector asymmetry is therefore occupancy evidence, not signed movement flow.
- `LocalOccupancy.sense()` is pairwise local body interaction within a `0.085` radius. It returns separation, crowding, and a same-flow headway speed cap; it does not keep a nest-annulus traffic history or feed an aggregate route direction into recruitment (`src/lib/agents/ant/LocalOccupancy.js:7-20, 35-95, 171-195`). `AntAgent.vx/vy` are updated by acceleration, then separation and swept collision scaling are applied before positions advance (`AntController.js:365-379`). The most defensible observer input for actual egress flow is the completed `{from,to}` movement segment passed to `GaitController.advance()` after collision handling (`AntController.js:371-392`, `src/lib/agents/ant/GaitController.js:48-70`), not `intentHeading`.
- The current movement loop deposits pheromone and records contacts before recruitment is advanced and a due spawn is attempted (`AntController.js:365-435`). A local-flow family would condition only future spawn initial headings; it should not alter ordinary movement, pheromone deposition, lifecycle, or material birth.
- `TrailPlateBirth` shares the fixed origin `(0.5, 0.72)`, but selects supported source-to-observation-boundary routes from a source annulus with radii `[0.055, 0.115]` (`src/lib/agents/ant/TrailPlateBirth.js:1-10, 332-390`). This selector is not the recruitment law. Its central anchor, immutable material, and the already-occupied union can preserve a similar envelope even if live routes rotate; downstream material comparison is informative only after a meaningful movement difference and must leave this selector unchanged.

## Static heading control validity

The clean interception point is the instance method, not a `random()` wrapper. A research runner can dynamically import the production class, replace the instance's `spawnNextAnt` before `init()`, call the original method, then transform only newly appended agents' `heading` and `intentHeading` before their first tick. This includes the initial spawn because `init()` dispatches through the instance method. It leaves accepted positions, clearance checks, population law, schedule, constructor draws, and heading draw consumption unchanged. No production edit is needed.

For the existing heading formula, let `U` be the accepted heading uniform draw and `h₀ = −π/2 + 0.3(2U−1)`. A wrapper can recover the same `U` as `(h₀ + π/2 + 0.3) / 0.6`, without intercepting the shared random function. Predeclare these four conditions:

| Condition | Initial heading for every accepted spawn | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | `h₀` | Reproduce current north-centered law. |
| Rotate 90° | `wrap(h₀ + π/2)` | Same spread, shifted center. |
| Reverse | `wrap(h₀ + π)` | Same spread, opposite center. |
| Isotropic | `wrap(−π + 2πU)` | Remove the fixed north reference while reusing the same draw. |

This transforms the *accepted-spawn heading sample*, not the position draw. The heading intervention itself adds no random draw. Because the generator is shared across spawn placement, heading, agent traits/clocks, spawn interval, and stochastic intention decisions (`AntController.js:451-474, 505-549`; `AntAgent.js:13-30`), exact RNG-call parity is expected only until changed trajectories alter a random-consuming branch or a later placement retry count. Record the first observed draw-stream divergence and the relevant time; do not claim full-run exact RNG parity. Beyond that point, the comparison estimates the natural total effect under the shared-stream coupling, not a pure geometry-only effect with independent randomness held fixed.

The reversed condition also points toward the nearer `y=1` boundary from `y=0.72`, while the baseline points toward `y=0`; the rectangular domain and natural retirement at its edge can therefore change exposure time (`AntController.js:378-382`). Record boundary exits/retirement and avoid reading a shorter southern route as evidence against founder direction. The ±90° control has balanced horizontal room and is a useful complement.

## Existing evidence and limits

### Stage A matched static controls

The 600-second matched-seed screen supports a **large continuing heading-condition effect on live spatial composition** in all three seeds. Applied heading distributions pass their manipulation checks. Across seeds, the late south fraction was 0.046–0.071 under baseline, 0.372–0.389 after a 90° rotation, 0.875–0.926 after reversal, and 0.339–0.403 under isotropic headings. Thus the result is not just a difference in the first recruit: every accepted spawn used its assigned heading law. These measurements support heading as a causal contributor to live spatial composition under the tested shared-stream policy; they do not establish that heading alone determines the colony envelope or that a rotated live distribution must rotate the material envelope. Full results and limits are in `analysis/stage-a-summary.md`.

The visual reading of the fixed-condition control figures supports only a static cross-run claim: imposed heading conditions produce visibly different global spatial distributions. Those figures do not establish spontaneous within-run regime formation.

The control changes are heading-only at accepted spawn: positions, spawn cadence and population law remain the production rules. Later RNG state, spawn times and accepted-spawn counts diverge as changed trajectories affect stochastic branches and clearance. The comparison is therefore a natural total effect under shared-stream coupling, not a geometry-only rotation with all later randomness held fixed. The reverse condition also faces the nearer `y=1` boundary, so boundary retirement remains part of its effect. Stage A establishes the heading-condition result for live distribution; it does not isolate a causal position intervention. Position is source-supported as a stable origin with small disk jitter, but its independent contribution was not tested.

The Oct 7 founder refinement changed low-evidence behavior clocks and trace dose while explicitly preserving recruitment, movement, and Plate qualification. Its fixed-field probe has one ant and no recruitment (`docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-07-founder-effect-refinement/README.md:1-3, 79`; `controlled-probes.json:11`). Its natural runs support that low-evidence behavior intervention only; they do not isolate spawn position or heading. Current guidance identifies the founder-refined baseline as `e38450ce11f99e63f5ab0cb6e8e8ee4281fcadd9` and says recruitment is an invariant (`AGENTS.md:22`, `docs/ant/README.md:30`).

The 2026-10-22 visual-turnover report records the repeated macro-envelope and notes that spawn and Plate-source anchors are plausible shared constraints, but explicitly does not isolate them. It is motivation for this test, not evidence that spawn heading caused the envelope (`git show deda54e6b3419af1abbfb6263adf81c4ac462a66:docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-22-visual-turnover-survey/REPORT.md`; source audit at the same commit). The older long-run exhibition report likewise lists fixed entry, source-to-boundary formation, and spatial saturation as competing explanations rather than selecting one (`git show c1332b8e686fd9f7099c8b33a8a017b6ce31cf63:docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-21-long-run-exhibition/REPORT.md`).

No production adoption follows from this audit. The observed live-distribution change supports heading responsibility for that outcome under this law, while an unchanged material envelope remains compatible with source-selector/history compression. The screen does not isolate all geometric effects: position was not manipulated, downstream RNG and spawn timing diverged, and reversal changes boundary exposure. These limits prevent interpreting the result as a pure rotation or as proof that heading alone determines long-term material form.
