# Stage A paired replay analysis

This analysis reads the frozen-state P/dtFeedback/dampingOff receipts and does not run or modify a simulation. Each arm is compared only with the other arms restored from that same complete state. Production and dtFeedback natural origins remain separate cases; their independently grown histories are not matched treatments. The loader refuses incomplete or protocol-mismatched cohorts and checks the compressed and uncompressed lengths and SHA-256 receipts for all 66 states, six history streams, frozen source copies and generated controller copies. It replays captured field operations against every recorded checkpoint hash before analyzing the states.

The causal gate requires the frozen-input SHA-256 to match the serialized complete graph, exact pre-state and pre-RNG receipts, pre-tick public-snapshot parity, equal natural-history state/RNG/snapshot, exact field reconstruction, the requested sample-time convention, a complete previous 60-tick travel window, timestep parity, equal entry sensing and agent state, equal adherence update and raw local cue, the same fresh-choice or held-intent state, and equal RNG calls through intention. Production-parity and origin-arm replay flags must also pass. Failed captures remain visible in the detailed output and are excluded from paired causal summaries.

For each ant, the analyzer retains entry and updated adherence separately. It records the signed local cue `u`, the arm's signed feedback contribution `v`, `u + v`, and the clamped local turn. Raw wide-sensor scores appear only when a fresh choice occurred; held intent is labeled as no fresh choice and carries no copied scores.

The first divergence is assigned in the frozen order: sense, choice, local turn, target heading, body omega/turn, then velocity or completed travel. Local cue cancellation, reversal, and same-sign survival use `1e-9 rad` to identify numerical zero. Each stage has its own eligible denominator. A body turn is the wrapped difference between heading before and after `turnToward`; its cap is checked against `4.5 * dt`. “Command error” in the report is explicitly the wrapped requested angular step from heading-before-turn to the post-crowding target minus the realized body turn.

Actual completed travel uses the postcollision `to - from` segment. The replay also records the source's `observedTo` endpoint, which can differ from `to` at a boundary; `travelled` is validated against the distance to `observedTo`, with `to` as the fallback when no observed endpoint is recorded. Actual and observed travel angles, tangent turns, change from pre-acceleration velocity direction, body-to-travel angle, collision scale, and per-tick chord displacement rate stay separate. The preceding 60-tick window reports actual and observed signed/absolute tangent turn, adjacent-turn sign agreement, path length, chord-to-path ratio, and chord displacement rate. A one-second net displacement rate is never labeled actual path speed, and the route summaries are not reduced to a curviness score.

The only strata are those frozen in [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md): entry adherence bands, coherence thirds, prior-omega sign with a `1e-4 rad/s` deadband, eight heading sectors, four half-cell raster-phase bins from fractional `(95*x, 95*y)`, and authored separation/front-crowding branch entry with the raw occupancy values retained. Position and prior travel details remain available per observation. These are descriptive engineering bins, not biological thresholds.

Counts and ranges are grouped by seed, natural origin, arm pair, and each frozen stratum marginally; declared empty bins are emitted with zero counts. Ant observations and repeated captures from one world are dependent; they are not treated as independent replicates. The report does not fit an aesthetic threshold or recommend a movement or field law. The result can qualify conditional one-tick read-side transfer on the captured raster; it cannot establish field-representation dominance, recurrent write/read effects, or total field contribution across divergent natural histories.

Run from the repository root after the complete matrix manifest exists, choosing a new output directory because the analyzer refuses to overwrite files:

```powershell
node scripts/ant-transfer-analysis.js docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-23-natural-field-transfer/capture/matrix-20261023/matrix-manifest.json docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-23-natural-field-transfer/analysis/reproduction-20261023
```

The first and second arguments select the matrix manifest and output directory. The command writes `REPORT.md`, uncompressed `results.json` (all state/ant/arm-pair raw measures and validation gates), and `marginal-summaries.csv` (every marginal stratum for all arm pairs). To reproduce the published compact package, gzip the new `results.json`, record its SHA-256 and byte count alongside the uncompressed SHA-256 and byte count, then remove the plain copy from the publish directory. The report includes fixed-seed/time/ant representative traces selected without looking at outcomes.
