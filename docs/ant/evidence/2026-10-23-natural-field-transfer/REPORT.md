## 1. Where natural field-to-turn improvement survives

**MATCHED-STATE REPLAY RESULT.** Six naturally grown 600 model-second histories produced 66 complete pre-tick states from three seeds and two origin policies. All 66 passed graph/state/RNG/snapshot parity, source receipts, tick convention and field reconstruction. The analysis independently reconstructed all 66 field checkpoints from six ordered streams. All 1,255 captured ant-state observations have complete P/dt/off arms.

Sensing, intention update, fresh/held choice and RNG through intention remain equal within each frozen-state comparison. The first P/dt difference is local correction in 1,251 cases; four have no analyzed difference. Among 1,168 nonzero local cues, production reverses the local-correction sign in 556 cases and dtFeedback in 93. This supports the delayed feedback responsibility on natural rasters, beyond the old analytic fixture. It does not say the cue identifies the correct path.

The difference reaches actual movement, with intervening attenuation. At the frozen `1e-9 rad` threshold, P/dt body turns differ in 350/607 production-origin and 604/648 dtFeedback-origin observations; actual postcollision travel angles differ in 351/607 and 603/648. Collision scale differs in two observations per origin. Exact first-divergence counts and thresholded transfer counts have different numerical-zero rules and denominators. Production-origin high-adherence states retain substantial body-cap occupancy even after a one-tick dt substitution; old intent/velocity are not reset. See [Gate A](GATE-A.md) and [full paired analysis](analysis/matrix-20261023/REPORT.md).

**Boundary:** one tick measures conditional signal transfer. Sustained future curvature is a separate natural-history result. Independently grown production/dt histories are not matched fields, and equal seed does not imply post-divergence lifecycle or RNG parity.

## 2. Raster responsibility

**CONTROLLED FIXED-HISTORY RESULT.** All 66 default-Gaussian half-cell challenge states passed phase-zero raster identity and all three movement arms' trace, complete endpoint, public snapshot and RNG identity. Each state has four phases × three arms. The same ordered physical writes and source dose/time are retained; writes and queries translate together while world poses/occupancy/RNG start remain fixed. Boundary clipping, storage and cap consequences are retained rather than normalized away.

There are 594 p00-to-shifted-phase comparisons. Interior cue comparisons are identical across the three movement arms: using one arm only, 3,360/3,449 comparable steering entries change and 323/3,449 flip sign. Absolute steering difference has median 0.01736 and p90 0.06990 rad. These are engineering signal contrasts, not correct-route scores. Do not count the three copies of the same sensed cue as independent evidence.

The effects reach movement with different transfer gains. In the all-local-reads-interior cohort, actual travel angle changes in 670/3,474 production and 1,882/3,474 dt comparisons at the numerical-zero threshold. Production absolute travel-angle difference has median 0, p90 0.001352 rad and mean 0.0008683 rad. dt has median 0.0001727, p90 0.014233 and mean 0.004598 rad. A high nonzero incidence can coexist with a very small median. Interior absolute body-turn p90 is 0.003169 rad for production and 0.032835 for dt. This is conditional sensitivity to physical-history/grid phase, not an observed cumulative free-running instability.

The wide-read denominator is explicitly restricted: 5,238/8,892 paired calls retain identical physical x/y, while 3,654 change query geometry and are not direct same-query raster comparisons. There are zero missing wide calls. Local/wide geometry, fresh/held decisions and numeric magnitudes stay separate. End-of-tick whole-arm RNG checkpoints match in 594/594 comparisons; per-ant cumulative counters are stream-alignment exposure, not proof that each ant caused a different draw count. Seven edge ant/capture pairs make no local field calls because every sensor point lies outside the domain. Their 63 shifted comparisons remain unqualified, with no raw-query mismatch invented from empty arrays. [Full phase analysis](phase/analysis/REPORT.md), [frozen protocol](phase/PROTOCOL.md), [66-state cohort manifest](phase/phase-cohort-20261023-manifest.json).

**Decision:** A3 is supported for two conditional links: delayed feedback interferes with natural cue transfer, and the default representation changes cues/turns when the same physical history moves relative to the raster. The latter becomes more visible when feedback no longer overwhelms the cue. This is not an interaction estimate of two new world laws and does not identify total field dominance. Grid-phase robustness is a remaining qualification issue; its ecological share and the responsible write/read subcomponent remain open.

No field kernel, radius, resolution, dose or read formula was replaced. The old failed CIC gate remains failed. Phase sensitivity on a fixed physical history is not an estimate of total natural field dominance, a correct-path oracle, or evidence that a broader/smoother kernel would retain directional contrast.

## 3. Movement candidate qualification

**CONTROLLED-PROBE RESULT.** Natural clamp exposure earned one new contrast: raw previous angular displacement without the extra ±2.2 feedback clamp. It kept gain, adherence, actuator, local limit and all other laws fixed. The preregistered family contains 144 three-second cases: P/dt/off/raw × six shapes × analytic/deposited96 × three seeds. The changed term activated in 3,131/6,480 raw-arm ticks and changed trajectories in 35/36 raw/dt pairs.

It failed qualification. Deposited mirrored-arc and S failures persist in seeds 19 and 61; seed 193 is mixed, including a tightMirror regression. For seed-19 deposited S, raw has about 0.0374 route progress and 0.0743 mean absolute cross-track error despite adjacent same-sign fraction about 0.983. The analytic S is tracked far better. A stable reduced scalar pole and persistent turning do not imply correct route tracking. See [Gate C](GATE-C.md), [all fixed cases](movement-qualification/REPORT.md), and the [equal-axis S comparison](movement-qualification/plots/sCurve-seed19-field-controls.png).

**Decision:** rawDisplacement is rejected as a production candidate; no new natural 600 s or 66-state raw replay was earned. dtFeedback remains a research contrast, with replicated natural improvements and known deposited-cue failures. It is not established as a unit repair, uniquely superior to dampingOff, optimal damping, or a qualified production law. No generic gain search followed.

## 4. Natural collective reuse and readable change

**MEASURED NATURAL READOUT OF PRIOR COUNTERFACTUAL HISTORIES.** Eight hash-qualified prior captures cover three 600 s production/dt seed pairs and one 7,200 s pair. The long cases are read only in three fixed 120 s windows. A frozen screen compares different ants' one-second, 10 Hz polyline routelets at one/two body diameters, with overlap, tangent-axis and curvature-sign agreement. A separately frozen follow-up anchors each trace at one fixed location. All 24 case/scale counts match the original screen.

Every case/window contains fixed anchors matched by at least three other ant identities. Across the three 600 s cases at one body diameter, dt support-anchor median absolute turn is approximately 0.718–0.740 rad and chord deviation 0.00436–0.00454 world units, versus production 0.076–0.083 rad and 0.000342–0.000346. Those are local geometric observations; visits are separated, not a continuously occupied long corridor. Pair/anchor counts are dependent and unnormalized for opportunity, traffic or lifecycle. The tiny-jitter control deliberately passes. Some maximum-support examples lie near nest/boundary constraints; geometric reuse alone does not identify pheromone causation, route recognition or following.

## 5. Material transfer and long-run consequence

**READ-ONLY NATURAL-STATE RESULT.** The unchanged selector has a current candidate in 27/33 captures of each origin. Supported cells without a selected candidate occur in six captures per origin. Support removes at most one chemical-qualified cell at these boundaries: counts agree in 32/33 production and 33/33 dt states. This localizes an unresolved loss after the supported mask, but the saved state contains only the selected winner, not each component/source/boundary/path rejection. It cannot identify shortest-path compression or rank geometric gates. Current candidate paths are longer in dt snapshots; that is not proof that upstream curvature is retained. Repeat/stability/gap gates also limit birth, and sampled states never reach the cap. See [material census and exact reproduction](MATERIAL-TRANSFER.md).

**LONG-RUN RESULT — prior provenance.** The exact prior one-seed 7,200 model-second pair remains the counterexample: production ends at 256 Plates / 77.34% union; dt at 247 / 99.20%. At ten minutes dt was slower to form material. Early slowdown therefore did not preserve long-run blank space. This is one seed pair of accelerated data, not a two-hour browser soak or a general law.

**IMPLEMENTATION FACT / DESIGN INTERPRETATION.** Append-only material union cannot reopen a cell. Identity, column multiplicity/depth, locomotion, visual presence, sound participation and provenance need explicit separate lifetime contracts if physical renewal becomes a requirement. Preserving identity or aggregate count is not equivalent to preserving top geometry or acoustics. Age is not burial; old IDs cannot simply be removed while preserving the current `m(L)` law. No erosion, aggregation, readout fade, cap change or persistence prototype is adopted in this pass. [Consumer contract](PERSISTENCE-CONTRACT.md).
