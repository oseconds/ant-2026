## Accuracy and physical limits

The model is a small-slope Newtonian precursor-film approximation. It has no true dry front, inertia, viscoelastic stress, elasticity, curing, gravity, contact-line pinning or fracture. An obstacle is a no-flux height-domain hole with a natural slope boundary. Its rasterized area varies with resolution; only the separated-pair case receives the full grid ladder.

N32/64/128 endpoint morphology differences decrease, and pair final-field timestep differences decrease. These are empirical refinement checks, not a proof of the continuum limit. Initial connection times remain step-sensitive; they are not quantitatively converged. W and b receive one fixed fine-grid sensitivity each, not separate full convergence studies. Halving the precursor changes background material and mobility as well as front regularization, so that effect is not a truncation-error estimate.

The three height levels are descriptive observers. A count of zero means no region above that level, not complete fusion. A one-component tail means persistence only to the fixed endpoint. Fluids remain able to move; no permanent solid was created. Negative excess `h-b` is allowed and measured; total height stays positive. The four-neighbor convention does not discover a natural material ontology.
