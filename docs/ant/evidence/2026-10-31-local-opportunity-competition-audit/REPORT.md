## Temporal order is interpretable locally but not a universal accumulation chain

All registered signed analysis strides, pair counts, actual elapsed-time distributions, Pearson/Spearman correlations and autocorrelations are preserved in [cases.json](analysis/cases.json). Positive stride means the second signal is later. Center-sampled and changing-X-region series are both reported. X-region B/C/D and signed changes are spatial means; feet and travel are separate spatial totals on exact X F cells, without cross-channel fusion.

For the longest non-entry D case, changing-X feet5 and ΔC5 have simultaneous Spearman `.978` (158 pairs), but at +5 analyses the correlation is `−.028` (153 pairs; median elapsed 5 s). Travel5 versus ΔB5 is `.057` simultaneously and `.386` at +5. ΔD5 versus accumulated D is `.238` simultaneously and `.322` at +5. These do not identify one uniquely ordered inscription sequence.

The disagreement persists across the objective examples. In the longest entry case, feet5/ΔC5 is `.484` at zero stride and `−.328` at +5. In the selected ΔB5 case, travel5/ΔB5 is `−.364` at zero and `−.518` at +5. The short selected ΔD5 case has only 15 observations; its +5 comparisons have 10 pairs and span a median 5.0833 s. These short, moving-region correlations deserve limited interpretation.

Autocorrelation is substantial: in the longest non-entry case, one-stride Spearman is `.891` for feet5, `.893` for travel5, `.958` for ΔB5, and `.995` for D. Overlapping windows, shared production ancestry, qualification changes, region drift and state accumulation confound lag interpretation. Production source establishes that actual contacts write C and pheromone updates B; temporal precedence here does not establish an additional causal process. No predictive model, significance test or fitted material threshold is introduced.
