## Working architecture, interaction gate and stop

**[Research interpretation]** A supports one new organism responsibility: an already-materialized local world conditions angular maneuverability. Existing autonomous sensing/intent, velocity, collision, secretion and recruitment paths turn that local response into later world differences. The run supports that integrated consequence; it does not isolate the gain of a particular descendant loop, prove locality superior to a uniform lower cap, or establish time-step robustness.

**[Research interpretation]** B is a distinct contact-generation responsibility, but in this seed its extra C/D does not change the Plate/layer-count input. Keep it as MIXED evidence rather than increasing the shortening bound to force formation. Contact timing could matter to existing physical excitation, but Audio Off provides no sonic claim.

**[Authored meaning]** “A surface limits how quickly a body turns” is simpler and less psychologically committed than “material makes a trail trustworthy” or “material causes hesitation.” Column count is still only a chosen conditioning proxy. These findings do not establish that ANT needs any additional material feedback or that repeated layering is a defect.

The strongest research shortlist is A, with B retained as the mechanical/contact contrast. Existing production10% speed and research-only chemical residence are independent responsibilities, not superseded by A. **The next smallest closed pass is A plus production10% slowdown against10% alone**, same A law and no residence. It should test complementarity, redundancy, antagonism and stall/lock-in, not morphology. Reuse the exact native10%-On calibration control where source/config remain valid; register one new treatment only. If A's distinct response is absorbed or combined behavior becomes pathological, stop rather than tune either law.

Residence interaction may be considered only after that first interaction remains useful and stable, and after deciding the material's chemical meaning. Do not immediately run a three-feedback combination. No interaction was executed here: discovery stops with one promising responsibility and one mixed contrast, before behavioral authorship expands into C/D/E/F.
