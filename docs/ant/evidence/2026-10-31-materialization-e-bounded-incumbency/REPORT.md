## Why the previous pass stopped

The prior recurrence used `F(t) ∪ (Q(t−1) ∩ E(t))`. At P2 anchor 17, incumbent-only bridge cells connected the nucleus to cell 4948, which was current F but outside fresh fixed capture B. The prior stop gate also required every Q-only cell to be absent from F. Those two requirements conflicted. The witness was mathematically consistent with that recurrence; parity had passed, so this was not a simulation or parity failure.

The present correction uses:

```text
B(t) = n0-connected component of F(t) inside Disk(n0, .15)
incumbent(t) = Q(t-1) ∩ E(t)
admissible(t) = B(t) ∪ incumbent(t)
Q(t) = n0-connected component of admissible(t) inside Disk(n0, .15)
```

Fresh structural members enter only through B. A Q-only incumbent may be absent from F (Class A) or present in F but disconnected from B (Class B), but it must be a previous Q member and current E-qualified. The predecessor report and stop witness remain unchanged in the predecessor package.
