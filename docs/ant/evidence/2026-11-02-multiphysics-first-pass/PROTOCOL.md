# ANT multi-physics broad first-pass protocol

Date: 2026-11-02. Research only. This record is written before the new candidate results. Each experimental family also records its own physical definitions, fixed cases and observer conventions before its interaction analysis.

## Question and authority

## Bounded work

1. **Wet film:** reuse the existing 25 runs, their budget/refinement receipts and fixed atlas. Check their manifest without rerunning the campaign. Preserve the distinction between pre-existing positive precursor liquid, a truly dry substrate, Newtonian transport, viscoelasticity and curing.
2. **Orientation:** first audit available trajectory data. Use actual realized movement, not foot positions or requested velocity as a substitute. Prefer existing current-production data. If those data are inadequate, one bounded, causally inert recording is allowed only with exact source provenance, zero observer RNG and baseline parity. A historical pre-G trajectory cannot establish current-production organization. Separate traffic, polar and nematic order, supported-loop winding, spatial boundaries and observer persistence. Synthetic known fields validate definitions. No orientation-to-deposition feedback is added.
3. **Air spaces:** use the smallest analytically interpretable acoustic model. A planar solid ring has no specified air volume or roof. An open-top groove is not a pipe open only at its ends. Reject lumped Helmholtz interpretation where compactness, neck, wall or radiation assumptions fail. Validate an explicitly roofed chamber/neck model and any distributed reference only within their declared regimes. All dimensions and excitations are illustrative, not inferred from ANT masks.
4. **Mobile fragments:** use isolated controlled rigid-body/contact physics with position, velocity, rotation, inertia, unilateral contact and friction. Include free motion, oblique contact, a friction null, arrest/obstruction and reverse-drive release where supported. A wall stop is not collective jamming; contact or inactivity is never converted automatically into adhesion. Check timestep, overlap and work/loss accounting. Do not manufacture ANT forces from FOOT_CONTACT.
5. **Other candidates:** evaluate all original seven and the later wet-damper, orientation and mobile-fragment proposals. Not every candidate needs code. Record a literature-grounded mechanism, missing state, a possible discriminating test, present ANT compatibility and a scoped verdict. Do not report a paper's success as an ANT experiment.

Independent candidate workers own separate scripts, tests and evidence subdirectories. A fresh reviewer will check the completed evidence and arithmetic independently of those implementations.

## Interpretation and stopping rules

`SUPPORTED`, `MIXED`, `NEGATIVE` and `INCONCLUSIVE` always attach to a named claim. Separate numerical execution, controlled phenomenon, natural ANT occurrence, material formation and acoustic capability. No universal threshold or persistent-object ontology is inferred from visualization or observer cutoffs.

Parameters, inputs, placement and analysis windows are fixed before each interaction campaign. Numerical or provenance defects may be repaired and recorded; outcomes do not authorize parameter changes to obtain appealing geometry. Weak or absent phenomena remain results. Do not expand a failed candidate into another physical family automatically.

For coupling, distinguish:

1. conceptual coexistence;
2. a stated physical exchange/constitutive law with units, geometry and budget responsibility;
3. an actually executed and checked coupled experiment.

Individually validated components do not satisfy level 3. No common material class, universal stiffness, solver, timestep or material unit is a prerequisite. A proposed interface must identify the actual mass, momentum, traction, velocity, pressure, energy, boundary or constitutive-state exchange and its conservation/dissipation obligations. A data label alone is not a physical coupling law.
