# ANT multi-physics architecture: broad first-pass investigation

Date: 2026-11-02. Research only. This pass compares physical responsibilities before choosing an architecture. It does not adopt any material process, solver or formation law.

## Decision

**MIXED. Defer a unified architecture decision.** The component evidence supports different, conditional phenomena; it does not establish a self-forming multi-physics ANT world. Several attractive identifications are specifically unsupported: a 2D material hole is not an air chamber; stopped contact is not a permanent bond; a behavior-derived director is not a material stress field; Newtonian precursor transport is not viscoelastic curing.

The earlier audio conclusion is **partly revised in scope**. Retained provenance, explicit spatial mechanics and a separately declared readout remain useful responsibilities. A sparse *stratified solid* network is not a sufficiently general formation state for actual liquid transport, fragment displacement/rotation or air compression. It remains one downstream elastic reference when the material premise really is independently moving laminae. The broad evidence does not choose a universal replacement or justify a new common solver.

## C. A void needs actual acoustic geometry

The [cavity study](cavity/REPORT.md) implements an explicit rigid chamber/neck circuit, a narrowband transient ODE, and an energy-consistent chamber–piston surrogate. It rejects applying that lumped model to the supplied open-top groove or broad-mouth recess. It does not assert that those open spaces are silent; their distributed/leaky response remains unestimated. Adding a roof to make an open/open duct changes the domain; its ideal 1,715 Hz reference is not the groove's measured resonance.

The accepted chamber is `40 × 40 × 25 mm`, with a `40 mm` physical neck and illustrative radii `2/3/4 mm`. Analytic breathing frequencies are `147.066/216.527/283.563 Hz`. The accepted response sweep is restricted to 1–340 Hz by a declared `kDmax ≤ .25` screen, not a universal approximation error bound. Internal Q is an assumed 20. The large-aspect-ratio end correction and compact-aperture radiation law remain approximations.

For the base 3 mm neck, the 1 Pa, 1 ms pulse's frozen-radiation ODE agrees with its exact trace to `4.33e-9` relative maximum pressure error; the energy-balance residual is `1.83e-5` of supplied work. Frequency-dependent radiation in the sweep and frozen radiation in that transient are explicitly different approximations. The far-field power check is internal consistency within the same baffled half-space model, not physical laboratory validation.

The chosen piston has `C_wall/C_air = 1.807`, so a rigid wall is not a suitable approximation for that surrogate. Coupled quadratic energy drifts by `5.98e-8` of initial energy, and pressure-force work agrees with wall-energy change within `1.45e-4`. Its roots `118.749/458.850 Hz` are mathematical lumped-surrogate results; the upper root lies outside the compactness screen and has no physical validation claim. This is a narrow **level-3 coupled check** of supplied air/piston equations, not a formed solid–air ANT world.

The short-neck and 1–5 kHz diagnostic outputs are preserved and excluded from accepted physical claims. Their source-validity repairs are recorded in [PREPARATION.md](cavity/PREPARATION.md). No aperture or dimension was selected for an attractive sound. No audio listening or 3D air solver was run.

**Scoped verdict:** **SUPPORTED** for the declared model's internal near-resonance/ODE checks; **NEGATIVE** for inferring a Helmholtz body from a planar mask alone; **INCONCLUSIVE** for current ANT cavity formation and actual open-groove response. Broad candidate verdict: **MIXED**.

## D. Mobility, contact and stopping are different states

The [fragment study](fragments/REPORT.md) runs 20 controlled cases of planar compliant disks with translation, rotation, inertia, unilateral spring/dashpot contact and a Coulomb-capped shear history. No foot event becomes a force. All masses, gravity, dimensions, drive and contact parameters are illustrative. No stopped body is converted into a pin or bond.

The head-on restitution moves toward the repulsive-only reference: measured `0.807959` versus `0.808099` at the finest step. The nominal unclamped dashpot parameter `.8` is correctly distinguished from actual restitution. In the oblique pair, friction generates about `-.236079 rad/s` spin in each disk, while the zero-friction null stays at zero. Angular-momentum drift is roundoff-sized, but its preregistered monotonic decrease with step size was not met. That failure remains visible.

The floor-supported disk approaches the ideal Coulomb stop near `.102 s`, then moves again under shear history. At the fixed `.14 s` endpoint, base-case speed and spin are `.03591 m/s` and `.14819 rad/s`. Endpoint sign varies with stiffness. This establishes braking/contact torque within the model, not stable rest or a calibrated physical rebound law.

The loaded row against a fixed end stop remains blocked and moves away when drive reverses. Crucially, the zero-friction row does the same. The wall geometry suffices; these data do not demonstrate friction-caused collective jamming, a natural arch, long settlement or permanent attachment. Contact-state criteria are sampled at 1 ms and do not exclude shorter dropouts.

The row also has a ceiling at `y=1.05 m`. It was present in the pre-run code but omitted from the initial prose, and that documentation defect is recorded in the fragment preparation note. No ceiling force appears in sampled contacts; minimum sampled clearance is `.05031 m`. The result remains explicitly confined geometry, not a freely formed bridge or reef. These metre-scale test bodies are illustrative fixtures, not a physical scaling of ants or local-X.

The energy ledger remains **INCONCLUSIVE**: signed bookkeeping terms are not a validated physical dissipation/work budget. This is a material limit on the interpretation, not a hidden pass. Timestep/stiffness checks constrain selected trajectories but do not validate every contact process.

**Scoped verdict:** **SUPPORTED** for basic translation/rotation, finite contact, frictional torque and reverse release in controlled fixtures; **NEGATIVE** for friction being necessary for this row's blockage; **INCONCLUSIVE** for stable arrest, collective jamming, long settlement and an ANT force connection. Broad candidate verdict: **MIXED**.

## Integration: the finding is an interface boundary

[COUPLING.md](COUPLING.md) gives the separate comparison table for all seven requested pairings. Among the proposed cross-process combinations, only the supplied chamber–piston surrogate reaches a narrow executed-coupling level. Orientation × wet transport, wet × fragments, cure × elasticity, wet × vibration loss, long granular settlement, and vibration × rearrangement are not executed coupled models here. Dry disk-to-disk contacts do exchange force and torque inside their own model, but they do not validate a wet/grain, curing or long-settlement process. No combination is promoted merely because its component scripts work.

The smallest common contract names a quantity/unit, geometric support, transfer law/budget, causal time and rejection regime. Depending on the pair, the actual exchange is mass flux; force/velocity; torque/angular velocity; traction; pressure/volume flow; boundary motion; or a constitutive transition with its energy/mass source. The contract does not require one material class, matrix, timestep or universal micro-bond stiffness. None is implemented.

Multiple processes and multiple rates are independent axes. Slow formation can coexist with a fast acoustic model, but impulsive contact/fracture/topology changes still need meaningful state/energy transfer. A fast or stable audio calculation is not evidence that the formation model is appropriate.

## What deserves deeper work

The highest-value material question remains the **substrate and mass-source premise**: is the intended world genuinely prewetted, or are finite doses supplied onto dry substrate? The existing 25.39% precursor sensitivity gives a specific reason to resolve it before calling the liquid calculation a robust formation law. No new film campaign is automatically run here.

Direction fields may be valuable as observations if their spatial/temporal support survives the registered controls. They require less new physical state than liquid/grain/air dynamics, but their existence alone does not justify precipitation at a boundary. Mobile matter offers a fundamentally different current-position state from frozen deposition, but this pass fails to establish its desired permanent settlement. Cavity acoustics is mathematically coherent when its geometry is supplied; current ANT does not yet generate that geometry. Cheap acoustic cost is therefore not a reason to prioritize cavities.

Wet-damping and later hardening remain an interesting challenge to the idea that more activity must mean more sound. No universal wet→silent→dry→ringing law is supported. Choosing that artistic/material premise would require a substance/state law and a separate controlled test, not an age-to-timbre switch.

The broad pass supports **keeping provenance, present geometry, physical process and acoustic readout distinguishable**. It supports **withholding a final architecture choice**. It does not support combining every candidate, adopting singleton formation, changing the decision register, or rewriting production.
