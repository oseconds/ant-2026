## Current production

`SurfaceField` records normalized compaction from actual individual contact positions and relaxes it with a 1,800-second half-life. That state is not measured pressure, liquid height, particle packing fraction or strain energy. `TrailPlateBirth` maintains chemical memory and selects authored source-to-boundary corridors, then freezes descriptor masks/material fields. `MaterialStratigraphy.addPlate()` inserts immutable masks into chronological cell columns and derives exposure/depth; it does not advect material or solve contact mechanics. Its authored coordinate scale and density/modulus ranges do not calibrate the new experimental fluids, grains or air geometries.

Actual realized movement does contain vector information. FOOT_CONTACT positions and authored strength alone do not provide a horizontal impulse, torque, water dose or physical friction coefficient. The new orientation screen therefore audits stored movement first and records a new read-only trajectory only because the available current-source packets lack the required temporal vector resolution. It does not reconstruct travel direction from foot locations.

The audio research commit has one inherited, default-off `AntController` research hook that is absent from production. - The three local-X worlds have 137–156 material layers, but only 964–1,070 solver nodes; the median layer has two nodes. There is one shared worklet, not a worklet per material Plate.
- All 110 one-node local layers have identical isolated equal-strength impulse PCM under the shared material fixture. They do not necessarily have interchangeable coupled vibration state, phase, compliance, burial, forcing or output contribution.
- The 96-to-32 binary occupancy reduction loses subcell geometry and can split eight-connected source material into separate four-neighbor acoustic regions. This is measured information loss in that compiler, not evidence for a new material process.
- Buried, never-directly-struck layers can vibrate through indirect coupling. Direct-strike count is insufficient to decide acoustic relevance.
- Continuous cost follows awake nodes, stencils, local links and excitation work. Descriptor count is not a reliable runtime proxy. Browser realtime viability remains a different question from a faithful offline render.
- Per-deposition independent boundaries and two descriptor-attached pickups require justification. Separate material provenance, mechanical degrees of freedom and world readout remain supported responsibilities. The measurements do not specify bonding, liquid transport, friction, air pressure or curing.
- The failed dormant-Plate modal route remains negative evidence for its tested coupled-state reduction. It neither proves nor rejects every modal, port, waveguide or other physical model.

The earlier preferred sparse stratified network is consequently a reference for a chosen solid-mechanics interpretation. It is not a universal material-formation law, and it cannot gain liquid/particle/air responsibilities just by renaming springs. A broader world can retain explicit spatial mechanics while requiring different state and operators in different processes. This is an architecture inference, not an implementation or adoption in this pass.

## Wet-film study at 796d5c7

[Original report](../2026-11-02-wet-film-formation-first-probe/REPORT.md), [validation](../2026-11-02-wet-film-formation-first-probe/VALIDATION.md), [fixed atlas](../2026-11-02-wet-film-formation-first-probe/ATLAS.md), [comparison arrays](../2026-11-02-wet-film-formation-first-probe/analysis/comparisons.json).

Manifest SHA-256 is `165c2336fd69849c53c6612829a3bf3237dcd40f147611804982373ddc4db19e`. The final new verifier checks the former shared daily index at pinned `796d5c7` and the other 115 files using the original verifier's exact convention. In that older convention JavaScript and `.gitattributes` remain raw bytes; most document/data text is LF-canonical. The new package's broader LF-text convention is not retroactively imposed on those receipts.

The implemented Newtonian lubrication law uses `h_t + div(q) = s`, `q = -h^3 grad(p)`, and `p = -Laplacian(h) + Phi'(h)`. Capillary/wetting energy and positive mobility govern conservative transport. This is distinct from both immutable deposition and the matched-rate heat Null. The controlled pair develops a finite-horizon raised bridge; a source-off single patch falls below all three observer height levels instead. Mass remains conserved, so disappearance above a level is not loss of liquid.

Maximum recorded mass error is `3.44e-15` dimensionless volume. The pair's final grid differences decrease from 0.2043% to 0.0655% normalized L1, but early connection time is not converged. Halving the precursor changes the fine-grid endpoint by 25.39%, including changes to the background reservoir and mobility. A prewetted substrate is therefore a substantive premise, not harmless regularization proven irrelevant. The experiment has no truly dry front or separate dry bodies.

The conditional ANT input uses exactly 5,677 recorded feet from the first 60 seconds of seed05 CONTROL. The dose, footprint, fluid units and one-second source bins are assumptions. No water, stress, force or moisture is measured. The model continues to redistribute liquid after activity stops. It does not produce a cured, stress-bearing bond or new acoustic resonator.

Keep its verdict **MIXED** within those scopes. Do not convert Newtonian transport into evidence of viscoelasticity, dry-body fusion, shrinkage, hardening, fracture, elastic sound, particle adhesion or a natural ANT water law.
