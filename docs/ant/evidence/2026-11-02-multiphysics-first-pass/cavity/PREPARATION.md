# Preparation and superseded diagnostic record

At 2026-11-02 03:13:29 UTC, an initial diagnostic run completed against the then-frozen [`PROTOCOL.md`](preparation/initial-short-neck-diagnostic/PROTOCOL.md). Its inputs were: 40 × 40 × 25 mm chamber; 12 mm physical neck; radii 2, 4, and 6 mm; `Q_internal = 20`; and the same illustrative air constants and pressure drive retained by the accepted protocol.

That run is preserved under [`preparation/initial-short-neck-diagnostic/`](preparation/initial-short-neck-diagnostic/). It is **superseded and excluded from accepted result claims**. After the run, source review caught that the chosen `0.8217a` neck end correction is the large-aspect-ratio limit, while the original radii gave `L/(2a) = 3, 1.5, 1`; the center case was not a sound use of that approximation. The accepted protocol changes only neck length/radius values for this validity repair and records no error bound for the approximation. There was no outcome-driven resonance tuning.

The accepted protocol also clarifies a model split already present in the script: the frequency sweep uses frequency-dependent compact-aperture radiation resistance `R_rad(f)`, while the constant-coefficient transient freezes it at `R_rad(f0)`. Their exact-solution checks are within the same frozen-resistance transient model; the frequency-sweep and transient receipts are not asserted to be identical transfer functions. A small undamped wall-piston ODE was added to verify quadratic-energy conservation and pressure-force work exchange for the compliance rejection example.

At 2026-11-02 03:19:02 UTC, a later corrected-neck diagnostic exported a 1–5,000 Hz sweep. The 5 kHz end had `kDmax = kL ≈ 3.66`, outside the compact/lumped regime, so that frequency-response CSV and its associated full-band outputs are preserved under [`preparation/full-band-extrapolation-diagnostic/`](preparation/full-band-extrapolation-diagnostic/) and excluded from interpretation above the accepted band. The accepted rerun is preregistered to 1–340 Hz with the study screen `kDmax ≤ 0.25` at the upper edge; all three registered resonance estimates fall inside it. This changes only the accepted frequency observation window, not physical parameters.
