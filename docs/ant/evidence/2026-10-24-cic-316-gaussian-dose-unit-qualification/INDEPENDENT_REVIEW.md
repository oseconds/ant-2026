# Independent review

**Verdict: supports with caveats.** The review checked the frozen unit-factor derivation, no-cap audit, deterministic artifacts, unchanged Gate 2 comparison, and the limits of the fixed-cue claim. It found the evidence supports proceeding to a separate movement shadow; it does not support production adoption or a physical chemical-unit claim.

The main caveat is the F-to-G tradeoff. G adds 14 wide winner errors relative to F. Straight and S analytic-zero false-steering counts do not increase, but their mean magnitudes rise from `0.00013234` to `0.00435829` and from `0.00018106` to `0.00650217`, respectively. Phase spread and mirror residual also rise. These should be retained as explicit checks in any later movement shadow.

The reviewer did not rerun tests or the build. The review is limited to the saved fixed-cue evidence and its claims; it did not inspect movement behavior, long-run cap/threshold effects, boundary conservation, or natural-world outcomes.
