## 4. Two minimal support-contract probes

The following predicates were specified before the independent reviewer computed their compatibility. The reviewer performed a read-only preflight before the formal Pass 4 plan entry; this timing is disclosed in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md). The parent then reproduced and recorded the exact same rules, without repairing geometry or pickups.

- **A — all-anchor sampled associated support with event reinforcement:** associated E at every event anchor AND strictly positive represented retained mechanical contribution after the start.
- **B — closing associated support with event reinforcement:** closing-frame associated E AND the same positive contribution.

**[Modeling hypothesis]** A asks for support qualified at all saved anchors and actually reinforced during this period. B admits later support if qualified at closure and reinforced during the period. Neither is simply unqualified traffic deposition. Neither is threshold-free in the broad philosophical sense: A makes an all-anchor decision and both inherit the authored E gates and observed 20 s event grouping. No occupancy 0.65, tenure 14 s, contact-count 4, morphology budget or score was fitted. Anchor agreement does not establish unknown intervening physical continuity.

Positive new reinforcement is a deliberately tested interpretation, not a requirement in the artist decision. Inherited local history may legitimately belong to a later event, and quiet maturation need not require new feet. Carry-in is not a defect, and zero represented increment does not by itself disqualify material. The negative outcome is limited to the tested contracts and their missing full event law.

### Accumulation counterexample

**[New diagnostic, logical fixture]** Four externally supplied closed events contain a connected, persistently qualified and reinforced region expanding through 16/32/48/64 logical cells. Both A and B commit 16/32/48/64 cells; their append-only union reaches all 64. Earlier layers stay immutable.

This proves only that **these WHERE predicates plus finite event windows do not themselves reject expanding persistent traffic**. It is not a natural G history, a simulated chemical/contact trajectory or a 600 s prediction. An independent EVENT rule could reject some supplied events; precisely that missing selectivity must be specified rather than claimed as a consequence of windowing.

## Local-accretion comparison and strongest counterargument

**[Existing ANT evidence, pre-G]** The old local-accretion shadow used E-like qualification, reset/rematuration, 20 s local persistence and two distinct cumulative foot-contact timestamps. It froze maturation batches rather than production resonators. Its late 64.18–65.13% grid coverage and 99.43–99.72% largest-component share are historical counterexamples, not current-G predictions. The rejected interior-endpoint candidate separately showed that more retained branches can increase redundant overlap without fixing event identity.

The present A rule differs: event association and the finite event window constrain it; mechanical reinforcement is measured in that window rather than as two lifetime contacts. Those differences are real. **The strongest counterargument to a negative verdict is that A identifies genuinely new, spatially changing contribution without an added fitted amount cutoff.** The current two-event data does not show natural late collapse of A or B. Such a collapse must not be asserted.

Nevertheless finite per-event masks do not explain why only some periods/regions materialize. Both tested rules remain tied to broad eligibility and supplied event windows. The logical accumulation null survives, and EVENT/open-world/closure selectivity has not been independently supplied. The present bounded negative rejects advancement of these incomplete rules, not the entire event-support ontology.

## Gate and next action

**Stop at Gate 2 for the examined formulations.** No full candidate survives the first-case design check, so additional seeds would test an incomplete or silently expanded contract. No seed generalization was run. No production implementation is recommended. This is not a proof that no event-scoped support rule can exist.

The conservative advancement criterion is an explicitly justified EVENT/commit/reuse policy that addresses the open-world question. It is a **modeling/design criterion**, not a measured physical fact or an inference that the artist requires a natural endpoint. Existing birth may be retained as an authored closure, and source/boundary conditions are not categorically forbidden if causally justified. Their inadequacy is not proved by the observed continuation of use. What is missing here is that explicit justification and a complete support/geometry contract; the WHERE probes alone do not provide it.

Gate 3 is not reached: fragmented probes do not by themselves require growing/merging acoustic domains. Gate 4 is not used as a request to choose a prettier mask or repeat the already answered role-separation question. These partial probes have not become two complete technically valid formation contracts for artist selection.

**Single next direction, if this line is reopened:** specify what makes a connection-history event eligible to materialize, and what makes it close/recur, independently of its selected path geometry and the observation boundary. Keep the atlas and both probes as counterexamples. Do not start with another occupancy cutoff or more seed runs. The new conditional mechanical-contribution measure may support later provenance/phenotype work, but does not itself supply the missing materialization law.
