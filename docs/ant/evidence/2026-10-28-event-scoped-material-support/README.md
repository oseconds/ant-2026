## Metric semantics

- **Census is not membership.** The per-event census is any coordinate touched by E/H/I/candidate ribbon, including ribbon-only regions. Outside census does not mean compaction/chemical is zero. Final ribbon is the selected birth's actual mask, not the packet's last candidate.
- E fractions are fractions of recorded anchors. Both/either-endpoint interval estimates and longest anchor spans do not bound unknown subsecond continuity. They use exact recorded timestamps, not filled/interpolated missing states.
- Absolute E history starts at the packet's first saved analysis and ends at the selected birth. It remains distinct from event-relative onset/carry-in/run duration.
- Field trapezoid integrals have normalized-state×seconds units. E-active statistics use E membership, not literal-positive field values. They are not calibrated chemical mass, contact energy or material amount.
- Foot metrics count original individual feet. Nearest-grid exact positions and 3×3 neighborhoods are separate. `[start,end]` atlas contact bins are explicitly inclusive; a contact at the start anchor precedes assignment of that tick's candidate. The field-only replay uses `(start,end]` because the starting field already includes onset-anchor contacts.
- Mechanical contribution is the difference between the exactly reconstructed contact-fed endpoint and its no-new-contacts relaxation baseline. It includes nonlinear saturation interactions conditional on fixed original contacts. It is not a closed-loop world intervention, exclusive event ownership, a conserved dose or a proof that a rounded zero means no input.
- Single-layer compiler fixtures use the same actual material/pickups, exact support masks and `edges=[]`. They are not actual emitted descriptors. A failed pickup is not moved. Raw 32-grid OR projection is reported separately from successful compile. Same ID is not propagation between disconnected components.
- Two-layer append tests verify retained overlap/L only; Nodule advance and full coupled acoustics are not performed. Spatial novelty is not required for a legitimate new layer.
- The expanding-region fixture is a logical counterexample to WHERE/window selectivity alone. Its events are externally specified. It is not a natural G trajectory, causal late-world test or prediction of 600 s filling.
