# Lifecycle architecture gate report

## Recommendation and gate

**Gate 1, architecture-selection scope:** prioritize **B, one history-inhibited opening**, for the next bounded closed-loop research implementation. It has a complete causal contract and passed an isolated source-state/decay fixture; the closed-loop busy-equilibrium counterexample remains open. This is a recommendation to test an architecture, not evidence of an ANT activity cycle or production readiness. No adoption or production implementation occurred.

B is substantially smaller and more explicit than inventing outside ecology, a resource meter or moving all entrances. It changes the role/sign of one existing chemical-history link: recent use of the approach reduces future admission; decay reopens it. It does not count ants, cull them, prescribe busy/rest phases, require new roles, erase material, or rewrite Plate commit. This is an authored hypothesis with a clear failure case: **another busy steady regime or merely lower flux**.

The strongest alternative is to keep the current world: local vacancy/return already exists under continuously busy aggregate traffic. Whether new global thinning would reveal more to a listener is unknown. The design preference for B is therefore conditional on its next matched-rate probe adding temporal/spatial organization beyond that existing local turnover. Do not implement B in production on this report.

No forced artistic choice is requested now. A is retained as a null/minimal outside ontology; C does not supply activity phases by conservation alone; D is a genuine spatial alternative but is not yet a compatible drop-in contract with the current Plate root. Gate2 would be premature because these are not two equally validated ways of achieving the mission. Gate3 would require evidence that the redesign's cost outweighs its value, which is not established. Gate4 is a real dependency for D, not a reason to block the single-source B research probe.

## Constraints that changed the design

**[Implementation fact]** Source disk, chemical recruitment observation and Plate root share a center but have different footprints. Initial entry/heading are already strong spatial priors. Moving entry to the boundary would change route evidence, residence, founder conditions and material eligibility at once. First research implementation must keep all these spatial inputs fixed.

**[Implementation fact]** Normal exit discards agent state. The world does not simulate outside paths, identities or pheromone. A strict finite larger-world interpretation would be a new architecture, not correction of an existing offscreen simulation. ColonyActivation is local chemistry only; its floor/ceiling mapping is one controller decision, not the natural population.

**[Existing ANT evidence, pre-G]** The forced60s zero-ant pass retained old columns, strongly decayed chemistry and modestly relaxed SurfaceField; it could even commit inherited qualified Plates. Those facts support unequal temporal arrows. They do not require naturally empty intervals or prove G absence/audio continuity. Current event research retained authored maturation/commit/reuse: descriptor closure does not close activity.

**[Implementation fact + limited inference]** Adopted material resistance remains `m(L)=1-.10*(1-exp(-L/4))`. It has no direct steering or source-recruitment input. If paths/speeds were otherwise fixed, slowdown could lengthen residence and raise occupancy. The actual pre-G A/B shows changed motion/contact/active-ant-time and mixed lifetime/material results; it is not a current-G macro-lifecycle suppression measurement. Do not amplify this law to make B work.

### 2. Stationary flux and finite-pool nulls

**[New experiment: analytic counterexamples]** Under an independent stationary M/M/infinity model, mean occupancy near20 gives `P0≈2e-9`. Under a K=28 independent finite duty pool matched to the same mean, binomial `P0≈5–6e-16`. The latter illustrates that a finite pool can empty even less often. K=28 is a deliberate null example using the existing configured ceiling, not a proposed conserved population or an optimization.

These are not estimates of ANT probabilities. ANT has dependent residence, placement blocking and changing fields; actual exit times were not fit to an exponential distribution. The result is logical: removing a target, preserving offered load, or merely conserving a finite pool **does not entail lifecycle phases**. Recovery and resource mechanisms must supply an additional causal responsibility rather than acquire the word circulation by naming.
