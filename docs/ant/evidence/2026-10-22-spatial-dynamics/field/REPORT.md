# Spatial field representation probe

## Result

Terminology: this report's “selector” and “score winner” refer to heading-option selection in `AntController.updateIntention`, not to `TrailPlateBirth`. No material selector intervention or new selector fixture ran in this field assay.

This controlled assay found phase-sensitive default-field cues across all three deposition paths. The preregistered selector gate was **not met**: only one of four route families had phase-paired score-winner changes, fewer than the required two. The two changes were the mirrored `R=0.10` routes at station `-0.04`, from the center bin (`0`) to the corresponding side bin (`+0.47` and `-0.47 rad`). The default Gaussian support recorded fallback dose on those pairs, but that does not satisfy the family-count requirement. The conditional cloud-in-cell alternative was not run.

This is a negative result for the gate, not evidence that the field representation has no influence. The exact local and wide sensor profiles, coherence, steering, and far-sensor amplitudes shift between the two fixed raster phases for straight, gentle, tight, and S-shaped routes. Most of those shifts do not change the highest-scoring selector bin.

## Measurements

Across the 16 direct-thorax records, requested dose averaged `2.085531` per path and stored mass differed by at most about `9e-8`; no cells saturated. All records retained exactly `0.5` of stored mass after `15 s`. There were 1,700 no-support-node Gaussian fallback brush calls, carrying total requested dose `15.4051` (about `46.2%` of all direct-path brush dose). The half-cell phase had 1,034 such calls and `9.3568` fallback dose, versus 666 calls and `6.0483` dose at the grid-node phase. This confirms the fallback path is frequent in this fixed narrow-path fixture; it does not by itself identify the downstream behavioral effect.

For the 24 paired direct-path readouts, only the two tight-circle pairs changed their selector score winner. Across those same 24 pairs, mean absolute phase difference was `0.124 rad` in local steering and `0.145` in local coherence; mean absolute difference in the normalized local `L/C/R` profile was `0.151`. The mean pairwise relative difference between the two phases' maximum wide-sensor signals was `29.2%` (maximum `48.2%`). These are distributions over the fixed fixture, not natural-run effect sizes.

The two bin changes had stronger local than far-score evidence. For `tight-positive` at `-0.04`, the winner moved `0 → +0.47 rad`; local steering moved `0.0462 → 0.2404 rad`, coherence `0.8440 → 0.9514`, and local total `0.05352 → 0.05690`. Its maximum wide-sensor signal changed only `0.03252 → 0.03019` (a `7.2%` paired relative difference). The mirror had the equal-and-opposite steering and bin changes. Conversely, straight `x/y` routes at stations `0.08` and `0.20` showed phase-paired local steering changes of about `±0.214 rad` while retaining the same score-winner bin. Thus the measured sensitivity is not confined to tight curves, even though only tight curves met the score-winner part of the gate.

The continuous gaster path and default-dab gaster path each had `0/24` phase-paired score-winner changes, while retaining broad local and wide-profile differences. Mean absolute local steering differences were `0.127 rad` and `0.116 rad`, respectively; mean normalized local-profile differences were `0.145` and `0.147`. Their mean relative far-maximum differences were `24.0%` and `20.1%`. These zero winner counts do not mean phase invariance: the exact sensor values still varied.

The forced production pose converts each fixed thorax path into a gaster-rear path with mean longitudinal offset `-0.02580` along the route tangent, mean absolute lateral offset `0.0000882`, and maximum absolute lateral offset `0.0002832`. Mean gaster source-path length was `0.46837` versus `0.46345` for the thorax route (about `1.06%` longer). This is an explicitly prescribed kinematic transformation using the production body-pose geometry, not a naturally generated ant trajectory. Comparing thorax-continuous with gaster-continuous deposition isolates that source-path transformation under the same field representation and dose integration; comparing gaster-continuous with default gaster dabs isolates the deposition-mode change on the same prescribed gaster path.

Across all modes, mass-weighted raster RMS distance from stored cells to the deposited source path averaged `0.00294` for thorax-continuous, `0.00295` for gaster-continuous, and `0.00290` for gaster dabs. Mean per-record fallback counts were `230`, `230`, and `77.5`, respectively; their fallback-dose shares were approximately `46.2%`, `45.6%`, and `47.1%`. Fewer dab brush calls do not mean less fallback dose because each dab carries a different slice amount. Mean occupied cells were `51.25`, `52.25`, and `47.25`; mean peaks were `0.0570`, `0.0592`, and `0.0641`. The dab raster is somewhat more concentrated in this fixture at matched requested dose, but the downstream sensor metrics above remain the relevant readout.

## Causal reading and limits

The probe distinguishes four links. First, the recorded source path is continuous and fixed; the gaster mode changes that path through `BodyPoseController` before deposition. Second, `PheromoneField.depositBrush` rasterizes each amount slice over its Gaussian support, falling back to one nearest node when no node lies within the radius. The `0.0045` radius is smaller than the `0.01053` pitch, so the paired phase changes how often this branch is used. Third, unchanged `PheromoneField.sample` bilinearly reads that raster, after which unchanged `AntController.senseTrail` and `senseLocalTrail` produce the measured wide and local signals. Fourth, unchanged `updateIntention` receives those exact local values; the deterministic fixture removes crowding, jitter, and score noise and places its final draw within the maximum-score interval. Its winner is therefore the production score winner, not a natural stochastic choice.

The assay supports a phase-to-storage-to-sensor-profile link for these narrow recorded paths, and two direct-path examples where that profile change reaches the selector score winner. The path-transform and deposition-mode controls show that winner changes depend on where and how the fixed path is deposited; neither alternative gaster path had a bin change in this readout set. The frozen gate failed, so this pass makes no candidate-field recommendation and changes no production qualification threshold.

It does not estimate how often natural ants generate these paths, whether they would follow them, or whether the cue differences alter colony-level outcomes. The route histories are prescribed; sensing is evaluated at three fixed points; only two raster phases are used; and no natural movement or stochastic multi-seed behavior is exercised. The older d0 selector/birth fixture is separate prior controlled evidence from commit `d0e07d0` (`docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-22-route-curvature-survey/selector/README.md` and `selector/route-selector-results.json`); it was not rerun here. Neither fixture establishes natural incidence or biological field semantics.
