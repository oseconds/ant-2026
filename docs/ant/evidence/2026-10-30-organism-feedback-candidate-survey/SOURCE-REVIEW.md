## Gate decision and limits

The neutral trace demonstrates actual native-path availability for each C–G responsibility. One independent 600 s screen per candidate is therefore defensible as a bounded research screen, with each candidate alone against the neutral control. F must retain its sub-grid source-relocation label. E's timer opportunities remain subject to later blockage. None is automatically adopted or ranked by output volume.

These worlds are H0 diagnostics: the production 10% speed law is Off and chemical residence is Off. They cannot establish complementarity or redundancy with either link. Do not rerun A/B, stack candidates, sweep parameters, or interpret post-divergence shared RNG decisions as matched downstream choices. Report implemented facts, measured state changes, downstream observations, and authored meaning separately. Plate/Nodule counts, field sums, and opportunity maps are not success scores; the existing rooted corridor formation remains the actual Plate selector, while max-D/X are observers. One seed and one rate do not establish generality, timestep convergence, a physical material property, or artistic success, as recorded in the [survey plan](PLAN.md#L28).

At this review point no C–G candidate outcome was used to make this gate recommendation. The only world evidence examined was the neutral observer parity above and the earlier, separately scoped A/B and chemical-residence reports.
