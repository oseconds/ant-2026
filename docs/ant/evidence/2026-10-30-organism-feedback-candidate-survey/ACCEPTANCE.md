# Independent acceptance review

## Verdict

Accepted as a bounded, one-seed research screen of C–G causal responsibilities. This is acceptance of the evidence package and its stated interpretation, not production adoption, a ranking of artistic value, or proof of generality.

The report distinguishes direct same-state consumer changes from downstream coupled-world observations and authored meaning. It does not treat post-divergence RNG paths, Plate identities, admission counts, or late-map distance as matched causal mediators. The brief D/E process overlap is disclosed, has separate world/RNG/output boundaries, and its offline analysis was rerun after completion; it does not invalidate the five treatment records.

## Supported interpretation

- **C, D, and G are promising research options:** the direct native paths are distinct and interpretable. C retains an already-read cue and also reaches existing source reinforcement; D changes a conditional same-flow headway; G changes Cartesian velocity response at an unchanged target. Their world consequences remain one-seed observations, and C is not a route-only effect.
- **E and F remain mixed without a negative causal conclusion.** E changes fresh-decision cadence, but the recorded minimum-clock increase is only a potential delay; exact realized per-agent delays and later blockage censoring are missing. F changes the actual source geometry and has a real fixed-path chemistry response, but natural relocations are sub-grid and their usefulness or necessity is unresolved. Neither label means its tested causal link failed.
- **D plus unchanged production 10% slowdown is a defensible next information-gain question.** It tests whether conditional pair headway remains distinct under the existing broad speed law. This is not an artistic winner or permission to combine mechanisms.

## Strongest null and remaining gaps

The strongest caution is that 600-second projected-pheromone normalized L1 separation is already large across all five candidate worlds (about 1.176–1.256), including F's tiny source displacement. Thus late-map magnitude does not distinguish useful responsibility. F is not a matched generic-perturbation control, so its result cannot establish chaos or a universal sensitivity baseline. The one-seed screens also lack a second-seed/timestep study, direct mediator-specific forks, an exact natural partner-spacing distribution, realized E delay/censoring records, activation-specific admission attribution, and perceptual or artist review. No admission delta is assigned solely to ColonyActivation; no formal near-stall study was performed.
