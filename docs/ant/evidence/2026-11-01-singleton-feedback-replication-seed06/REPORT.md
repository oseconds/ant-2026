## CROSS-SEED INTERPRETATION

**SEED-DEPENDENT FEEDBACK SENSITIVITY.** The second seed had nontrivial direct exposure and its B/C/X landscape and normal production births diverged from its matched CONTROL. Yet the paired aggregate signs reversed relative to seed 20261005, and the time-window paths differed. Feedback sensitivity is therefore not unique to seed 20261005, but this two-seed result does not support a common directional response.

## NOT ESTABLISHED

This replication does not establish singleton-only formation as correct, production Plate semantics, B/C lamination/coalescence/exclusion, a capacity law, material-property differentiation, Residence interaction, or a universal response across seeds. No actual research Plate was created; the only branch change from production is the previously authorized default-off layer-count hook. No third seed or actual-Plate integration was run.

Smallest justified next question: should the seed-dependent response be treated as an explicit condition in any later feedback integration, or should one more matched seed be authorized to characterize direction variability first?
