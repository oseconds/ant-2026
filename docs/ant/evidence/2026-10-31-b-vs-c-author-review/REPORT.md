## What the histories foreground

**B foregrounds accumulated qualified chemical history.** Its strongest non-entry opportunity remains close to its channel winner: median within-channel ratio `.891–.907` across seeds. B's median rank autocorrelation is `.610–.648` at 5 s and `.065–.162` at 20 s. Its level has weak/negative association with recent travel5 (median Spearman `−.289` to `−.276`), while signed ΔB20 follows travel20 more clearly (`.572–.578`). Thus B can persist through quiet recent travel while its longer-window change still reflects recent traffic. Its strongest choices usually remain entry-local, but it supplies comparatively strong non-entry alternatives.

**C foregrounds contact-written compaction.** It supports long non-entry examples (the selected longest C track spans 184 s), yet its non-entry strength is lower relative to its winner: median within-channel ratio `.513–.572`. C rank identity changes faster than B's at 5–20 s, while C level and signed change relate closely to recent contact (median Spearman `.655–.671` for C level vs feet5, `.831–.845` for ΔC5 vs feet5). C also remains present during quiet-foot intervals. Its 1800 s native half-life does not by itself establish lock-in.

Both channels yield finite, connected X captures within `.15`, with median adjacent X Jaccard `1` for each channel and each seed. Their local structural substrate is often shared: reciprocal-nearest `.025` matches have median X Jaccard `1`; about `.584–.632` are exactly the same capture. But the strongest choices differ more: across 588 shared boundaries per seed, winner-center separation has median `.030–.032`, winner-X Jaccard `.779–.785`, exact same-X frequency `.031–.037`, and winner-rank Spearman `.166–.173`.

Existing material is common beneath both episode types, not a preference test. Median prior-material overlap ranges `.500–.857` for B and `.545–.857` for C across seeds. The selected technical panels show revisiting, extending, and less-layered local structures descriptively. Neither channel was rewarded or penalized for that overlap.

The feedback-loop concerns remain inferences only. B would place future episode selection close to the existing pheromone-routing pathway (`pheromone → B → selection → material → movement/material feedback → later pheromone`). C would tie selection to direct contact inscription (`FOOT_CONTACT → C → selection → material → movement/material feedback → later contact`). Neither loop was integrated; instability or mechanical lock-in was not tested.

These observations retain the prior classification **RESPONSIBILITIES DISTINCT / BOTH PLAUSIBLE**. B more strongly preserves non-entry competitor strength and rank identity; C more directly represents contact inscription and recent contact-linked strengthening. The visual packet is for author judgment about which responsibility should mean future materialization, or whether to keep both open.
