# Broad environment to one finite proposal: EMERGENCE only

## 1. Decision

**[Architecture inference]** Keep environment-side proposal generation separate from future UPDATE and process continuity. The useful production machinery is not exhausted by source-to-boundary semantics: thinning plus lossy route projection can still generate a finite connected structural interpretation without those endpoint tests. The present frozen comparison demonstrates computational availability, not a source-free material law.

**[New frozen-state measurement]** Three preregistered countermodels all return finite E-contained connected paths at ten saved states. They expose different limitations:

- **A, global skeleton diameter:** selective and root-free, but inherits a large global extent. COMMIT geodesic lengths are .9700 and1.0437 world units. There is no intrinsic local bound.
- **B, greatest finite chemical peak–saddle lobe followed by its spine:** actual continuous fields contain finite contrast domains. COMMIT bounds contain43/13 cells and yield19/10-node paths. However, the winning proposal has zero overlap across one adjacent1s transition in **each** event. The dominant essential peak is excluded by definition, not disproved as material history.
- **C, maximum supported digital chord:** does not need skeletonization or authored terminals, but authors straightness and global maximal span. One preregistered single-cell deletion changes the P1 winner's Jaccard to .0674. Raster line semantics remain untested alternatives.

**Working recommendation:** preserve root-free backbone extraction as reusable machinery; do not adopt A/B/C as the next selector. Narrow the next research question to whether chemical peak–saddle domains are repeatable spatial objects independently of the *global winning-lobe ranking*. This is the only computed family that supplies a local termination relation beyond an extreme pair in the whole component, but that relation has not earned material meaning. No new field is shown necessary; no absence of intrinsic scale is proved by these three constructions.

**[Artistic interpretation]** No naturalness, material identity, viewer readability or preferred Plate form was judged. Lossy omission remains allowed. A valid history's exclusion does not make it false.

## 5. Countermodel definitions and responsibilities

### A — intrinsic global structural spine

**[Research countermodel]** Apply unchanged production thinning to all E. Within every eight-connected skeleton component, compute exact weighted all-pairs shortest paths. Select the pair with greatest finite shortest-path distance globally; select the lexicographically first pair within1e−12 ties and the production-style lower-parent tie for path reconstruction. This is exact Dijkstra from every node, not a two-sweep tree approximation on a cyclic graph.

- **Location:** global maximum geodesic separation; no designated source. **Extent:** two intrinsic extreme nodes, constrained by component connectivity. **Finiteness:** finite graph plus one simple path; **not a local upper bound**.
- **Omission:** other components, branches and alternate paths can remain valid without inclusion. No recall objective.
- **New authored assumption:** longest connected structural span deserves the one proposal. No Lmax, radius, area or branch count. Connectivity and row-major ties remain authored representation choices.
- **Portability:** no source/target constants are read by this operator. Without the annulus it still has endpoints. Without a physical boundary it still terminates at graph extrema, but those extrema may be observation-window-censored. Changing the observation domain may change the global winner.
- **Huge E:** can span the entire skeleton's diameter; no guarantee of locality. On a filled window it also inherits unthinned rim handling.
- **Future UPDATE:** can be placed after it; may resist winner switches, but cannot supply a missing material reason for global diameter. UPDATE was not added.
- **Falsifier:** needed local proposal scale is absent, or representation changes/late history cause unacceptable extreme switches. **Cost:** all-pairs Dijkstra is an offline diagnostic, not a production runtime recommendation.

### B — chemical peak/saddle domain, then a structural spine

**[Research countermodel]** Build a superlevel merge tree of existing B-memory on E under eight-adjacency. Process equal values atomically; connected maximum plateaus are one peak. At a merge, elder is the higher birth peak, with lower row-major peak cell for equal births. A nonessential peak dies at the merge level; contrast is birth minus death. Select greatest finite contrast globally, lower peak-cell tie. Its domain is the strict-superlevel E component containing that peak (`B>saddle`). Thin that domain and apply A's exact spine procedure.

- **Location:** greatest finite relative chemical contrast, not maximum raw B. **Extent:** last domain before it merges across its saddle into an elder domain. **Finiteness:** a finite connected superlevel lobe plus one spine.
- **Essential roots:** each E component's surviving peak has no internal death level and is excluded. Single-peak/unimodal E may produce **no proposal**. This is a declared semantic omission, not evidence that the strongest history should never materialize.
- **New authored assumptions:** peak–saddle contrast as salience; greatest finite lobe as single representative; essential-root exclusion; eight-adjacency/strict inequality/elder ties. No fitted prominence cutoff, neighborhood radius, morphology cleanup or desired area.
- **Actual inputs:** B is existing pheromone-derived EMA, not chemical mass or new state. E supplies an already clipped domain. Mechanical D is inspected separately, not multiplied into a new score.
- **Portability:** origin is an intrinsic maximum, termination an intrinsic merge relation. It needs no source coordinate or boundary-target test. Nevertheless E clipping can remove the saddle/elder and change a lobe into an essential root. This is not observation-window invariance.
- **Huge E:** may have a huge lobe or one essential peak and no proposal. Locality observed here is not guaranteed.
- **Future UPDATE:** changing winners might justify later incumbency, but UPDATE cannot validate peak meaning; no rescue performed.
- **Falsifier:** domains are raster/clipping artifacts, or apparent continuity exists only after arbitrary prominence/size filtering. Global ranking jitter and domain incoherence must be separated before rejecting/advancing the whole family.

This is a **superlevel contrast lobe**, not a watershed catchment and not a natural material event. Its finite region only bounds the spine. No lobe cells become Plate membership directly.

### C — maximum supported digital chord

**[Research countermodel]** Enumerate all unordered E endpoint pairs. Rank squared Euclidean separation descending, then endpoint indices. Generate the canonical lower-index-to-higher-index Bresenham eight-connected digital line; retain only a line whose every sampled cell is in E. Choose the first longest valid pair, recording co-optimal pairs.

- **Location/orientation:** globally longest line of digital sight inside E. **Extent:** two extreme endpoints of that supported line. **Finiteness:** finite endpoint set and finite sampled segment.
- **Omission:** curved/branched/parallel history is allowed to stay outside. No skeleton degree/cycle readout.
- **New authored assumptions:** straightness/global maximum as representative; Bresenham cell convention and endpoint ties. No fixed angle list, radius or length cap.
- **Portability:** has endpoints without source or observation-target semantics, but a large connected/convex E can yield almost the window diagonal. B/D affect it only through E qualification.
- **Representation risk:** a Bresenham path can pass diagonally between missing orthogonal cells. Supercover/continuous-cell-union segment semantics were **not tested**. No claim of a physical continuous corridor follows.
- **Future UPDATE:** stateless initialization is separable from incumbency, but cannot interpret curved history naturally. **Falsifier:** cell-crossing convention or a small support edit changes orientation/location substantially. Brute-force cost is diagnostic only.

### Two additional design directions, deliberately not prototyped

**Consolidated-core ridge.** Existing B/E history can describe consolidation, but selected I and rejected support do not show a useful separation in the present descriptive check (§7). A new tenure/volatility threshold would re-author field membership without a justified selector. This family is parked; temporal inputs alone would not be UPDATE, but here they do not yet explain location or finite extent.

**Mesoscopic structural packet.** A local maximum plus finite radius/node count/branch budget would supply location and termination explicitly. It is technically coherent and could be an artistic world law, but its mesoscopic scale would be **new authored spatial semantics**. No independently grounded scale was identified. It was not implemented or smuggled into A/B/C.

Nucleation maxima alone rank origins but do not terminate a proposal. Direction/gait/stratigraphic inputs were not added merely to enlarge this inventory. Three computed contrasts are sufficient to test the present dimensional-reduction and intrinsic-domain questions.

## 8. Portability and surviving architecture space

**[Architecture inference]** All three operators are defined without the source annulus tomorrow. All three terminate without naming the observation edge as destination. But the frozen activity was generated by H0 with its privileged source and north-facing bias. No result here demonstrates an unbiased world or boundary-independent outcome. E itself, and B's saddle hierarchy restricted to E, are observation-clipped inputs.

The strongest surviving null is therefore narrow: **a broad qualified state can supply one lossy structural projection; event tracking need not grow space**. Current production remains the only implemented proposer. Its useful structural principles can be reused, while source/boundary authorship stays an explicit unresolved replacement responsibility.

No new Lmax, fixed event radius, branch budget, area target, cleanup iteration count, prominence or volatility threshold was adopted. There are nevertheless new authored choices: global diameter, greatest finite contrast, essential exclusion, digital straightness and raster ties. Eliminating fitted numbers does not eliminate authorship.

Per-event narrowness is demonstrated; long-run union coverage, repeated COMMITs, layer multiplication and600s stability are **not**. Thin source-free paths could still accumulate broadly. The local-accretion counterexample is not resolved by calling these proposals finite or1D.

## 9. Smallest next closed pass

**One question:** are finite B peak–saddle domains reproducible spatial units, with the observed switches chiefly caused by single-winner ranking, or are the domains themselves representation-fragile?

Use the same ten exact E/B states, no new nature. Predeclare two domain conventions: existing8-adjacency and4-adjacency as a corner-contact sensitivity control. Compute all finite lobes; report spatial correspondence, peak/saddle identity and essential/nonessential changes without selecting a “good-looking” subset, tuning a cutoff or carrying incumbents. Distinguish domain availability from the separate max-contrast ranking. This is a landscape representation test, not UPDATE and not a material rule.

**Falsifier/stop:** if domains themselves lose location/extent under the declared cell-contact convention, or only post-hoc size/prominence filtering rescues them, park the basin family. If domain families remain coherent but global winners switch, stop with a domain-availability result and leave proposal ranking/artist meaning open. Do not add hysteresis, new state, BODY or a production selector in that pass. A positive result would still not prove long-run selectivity.

This prioritizes the only new local termination relation observed here. It does not require artist selection of a prettier atlas panel. If that relation fails, explicitly authored packet scale or continued use of the current rooted selector remain honest alternatives; this report does not invent a hidden natural scale to avoid that choice.
