# Plate body-generation exploration

## Outcome

**One construction family survives as a technically viable research direction: finite connection sections whose radii read local chemical history (W), retained together with the uniform-mean control U.** This is not a natural WHERE law, a production candidate acceptance, or an artistic winner.

The more important negative is that **finite scaffold does not by itself provide selectivity**. Taking the whole non-pendant alternative-path set already makes a broad body at the minimum existing width, before environmental deformation. C0/C1's selectivity problem can be moved into the scaffold rather than solved.

The exploration supports a useful responsibility split—structural scope, construction, and whole-body character—but not the stronger claim that existing fields uniquely determine a natural material body. Most explored concepts either remain a path-derived sleeve, reproduce broad scaffold geometry, or rely on a raster enclosure interpretation that is not physically established.

Human judgment is pending. The [atlas](atlas.html) is a technical comparison, not the ANT renderer or an aesthetics benchmark.

## Definitions and candidate reduction

All new generators read the same frozen event. `r0=.012`, `r1=.030` reuse existing TrailPlate half-width bounds. This does **not** validate a chemical-to-radius law. The linear relationship is authored. No fitted constants, eventwise maximum normalization, percentile, area budget, morphology cleanup or island deletion was used.

Let I be actual selected scaffold cells, T the prior non-pendant terminal-path set, B chemical memory and C raw compaction. Distances use96-grid node coordinates divided by95; masks are evaluated on that source grid. The atlas maps these cells consistently to640×640 with nearest-neighbor display.

### Family 1 — finite structural sections

- **K:** fixed `d(x,I)<=r0`, a control that withholds history from geometry.
- **S:** `d(x,I)<=r0+(r1-r0)B(x)`. The ambient field at a proposed body coordinate conditions its contour. P2 yields one isolated4-connected cell; it remains in the artifact.
- **W:** union of discs centered at each `i in I`, with `r_i=r0+(r1-r0)B(i)`. History belongs to each structural cross-section; the constructed body is the union of those sections. This avoids S's detached lobe by construction rather than removing it afterward. It still produces a path-derived sleeve.
- **U:** identical construction and scaffold, but every radius is the mean mapped W radius. This separates local radius assignment from mean widening; it does not match raster area exactly.

[Modeling interpretation] W is coherent if a finite connection is allowed to organize material whose cross-section records local history. Its connectedness follows from overlapping constructed sections and the existing scale, not a discovered natural material process. The body may extend outside the instantaneous qualified E set.

### Family 2 — retain alternative connections as one body

- **T control:** `d(x,T)<=r0`.
- **R:** same scaffold with chemical contour `d(x,T)<=r0+(r1-r0)B(x)`.
- **M:** same construction with raw compaction C instead of B.

[New exploratory observation] T is already1069/1275cells, versus C0's901/1172. R adds only66/92cells. R/T overlap is0.942/0.933. M and R overlap0.945/0.941. Scaffold breadth/minimum width dominate these particular bodies. No compaction gain or narrower floor was tuned to make them distinguishable.

[Modeling interpretation] The concept of preserving alternative connections remains meaningful, but freezing this entire explanatory hull as one body is not a demonstrated solution to C0 selectivity. R/T stay in the atlas as a challenge set; they are not technical survivors or proven load-bearing redundancy.

### Family 3 — interstitial lamina

**L:** R plus every bounded component of the4-neighbor complement of the terminal scaffold. This explicitly proposes spanning interiors between reconnections; it is not hole cleanup of another candidate. An8-neighbor complement is retained as a convention sensitivity.

[New exploratory observation] Raw pocket masks have4/8-complement Jaccard0.402/0.503. They contain many tiny raster pockets. Filling them adds only34/42cells beyond R, with final R/L overlap around0.971. This is a weakly differentiated, representation-sensitive membrane premise. L is not advanced. No pocket is selected by size or appearance.

### Family 4 — history characterizes an already bounded body

**Q:** K geometry, with the existing production coefficient formula evaluated over that body:

`stiffness=.65+.5*mean(B)`, `damping=.25+.2*mean(B)`,
`density=1+.2*clamp(mean(D)/.08,0,1)`.

This changes the averaging footprint, not the formula or geometry. P1 stiffness is 0.862386 and damping 0.334954; P2 stiffness is 0.887199 and damping 0.344880. Density stays1.2 in both: the existing support normalization saturates. Q shows a compatible role separation, but is not a new material ontology or evidence that the coefficients sound better/different here. No local map was introduced.

## Counterexamples and revision of the simplification

- **C0, current G:** finite/compiler-valid individual commits still accumulated to62.77% union and broad repeated layers. Mathematical finiteness per event is not long-run selectivity.
- **C1, current G:** sampled attachment retained96.45%/99.74% of C0. More temporal ancestry did not solve body extent.
- **Local accretion, pre-G:** the historical `13ffc9f` README shows locally qualified20s batches, without source/boundary/skeleton, converging to about64–65% coverage. Its permanent union follows changing traffic footprints. This is a historical counterexample, not a current-G prediction.
- **Current selected ribbon:** W changes cross-section and recovers no new alternative-path identity. It must not be presented as resolving the sustained-route omissions of production I.
- **Full terminal set:** R/T demonstrate that copying all graph-supported alternatives can recreate broad filling even without testing E directly at the final body cell.

[Research interpretation] The working simplification needs an extra responsibility: **which finite portion of connection history belongs to one body**. Environmental shaping can make that choice legible, but cannot supply it automatically. Selectivity is still partly upstream in event/scaffold organization. Current construction also remains window-censored. No new field or score is warranted merely to hide those choices.

## Artist gate and next closed pass

The shortlist is **W/U as a construction comparison**, with Q retaining an alternative whole-body responsibility. There is no selected final renderer/body design. Ask about material meaning rather than prettier masks:

1. Is a material layer organized around one connection with locally varying sections a valid body, even when it remains path-like?
2. Should sustained alternative connections belong to that same body, or should their broad hull remain evidence/provenance without all becoming matter?
3. Does the local W/U difference matter to the desired reading, or is a simpler uniform section plus whole-body character sufficient?

**No new closed long-horizon pass is recommended before that judgment.** The existing data already falsify a claim that finite scaffold alone solves selectivity, and there is no basis to tune W for a desired look. Technical compatibility is enough for this exploration's stop.

If the connection-section interpretation is explicitly retained, the smallest subsequent closed pass would freeze W and U unchanged and inspect **one predeclared later actual commit with repeat/overlap contrast** from existing current-G evidence. Its question would be whether local assignment adds stable source/solver geometry beyond the mean control while keeping immutable re-layer semantics. No new600s natural run is initially needed. Stop if the comparison is merely mean widening, relies on unsupported frame closure, or requires repair/retuning; do not progress automatically to production. This is conditional design advice, not a scheduled or executed experiment.
