## Review focus

- Float32 addition, cap loss, outside-domain rejection and collapsed boundary nodes must not be mislabeled exact moment conservation.
- The analytic cue is a controlled comparison model, not natural-path truth. Lower global error must not hide mirror/S/straight regressions.
- Original query geometry, source dose and sequence remain fixed. Contrast/peak/support changes are reported independently.
- Equal seed after a free-running intervention would not imply RNG/lifecycle parity.
- No movement outcomes are evaluated unless the complete fixed-cue gate passes.
