# One finite materialization scope within wider environmental history

## 1. Decision and accepted premise

**[Artistic decision, supplied for this pass]** Environmental/connection history may be wider than any Plate. Valid history may be included, omitted, later materialized, or never materialized. Omission is not by itself failure. This resolves the architecture synthesis's former completeness question; this report does not ask it again.

**[Modeling interpretation / working recommendation]** Use a **commit-time lossy representative scope** as the minimum working contract. A changing selected connection configuration may belong to one mature candidate without preserving every earlier branch. At the existing commit, close the currently selected finite connected representative. Do not automatically union earlier selections or all supported alternatives. Branching inside the selected representative is allowed; it need not create another Plate per branch.

This is a choice about what the materialization represents, not a discovered natural law, adopted production change, or permission for arbitrary omission. Selection must have a declared positive procedure. Current I supplies one existing, deterministic procedure for comparison. Its source/boundary authorship remains explicit and prevents claiming a final open-world scope law.

## 4. Three scope families, without a Cartesian recipe

### A. Evolving representative, membership closes at COMMIT — recommended working semantics

- **Initiation:** the existing selector opens a qualifying candidate lineage. No new event trigger.
- **Positive inclusion:** one selected finite connection organization at the current analysis; current I supplies an existing deterministic parent-route projection. At COMMIT use its current member set.
- **Same materialization:** the existing candidate continuity/maturation relation groups successive representations. It does not preserve every node or branch.
- **Omission:** valid H alternatives, exploratory appendages, and previously selected pieces absent from final representation remain outside this layer. No recovery obligation.
- **Branching:** a selected representative may branch. A branch not selected is not fake and need not receive a separate Plate. No one-ID-per-branch expansion.
- **Termination:** structural membership remains revisable until the existing COMMIT, then closes. No later update to the old Plate. Reissue remains the current separate policy.
- **Finiteness:** one finite selected connected graph rather than temporal union/all environmental support. In the current proxy, the observed finite grid and terminal paths bound it. This does not prove physical body closure beyond the observation window or bounded total accumulation.
- **Existing information:** current qualified graph, candidate clock, deterministic root/parent/terminal projection. No raw motion, new field, new RNG or strata read.
- **New authored commitment:** materialization may preserve the selected configuration *at commit*, even when earlier configurations differed. The selector's published projection—not history completeness—explains inclusion.

This is not a claim that arbitrary subset choice is natural. The declaration of selector, input graph, connectivity, tie rule, terminal rule and closure moment makes the loss inspectable and reproducible. The source/boundary terms are explicit limitations, not physical necessities hidden by the word lossy.

### B. First coherent capture, membership closes at START

- **Initiation/inclusion:** capture the first selected I when the existing lineage begins. Freeze that structural membership for a possible later commit; material COMMIT time itself remains unchanged.
- **Same materialization:** later history witnesses maturation of a previously captured organization. Exact F is the strict reference, without branch matching or geometry deformation.
- **Omission/branches:** later branches and reconfigurations never automatically join; initial branches remain members even if current selection moves elsewhere.
- **Termination/finiteness:** membership closes at capture, descriptor materialization still occurs at existing COMMIT. A finite first graph cannot grow by later history accumulation.
- **Existing information:** first actual I and existing event clock. No shape cleanup or threshold.
- **New assumption/cost:** first detection has privileged material identity, and the clock for the evolving candidate may mature a structural object that is no longer its current selection. An abort/revalidation or deformation rule would be another authored decision; none is added here.

**Disposition:** coherent if the artwork intends an early trace later fixed, but not the minimum continuation of current qualification semantics. F's changed current-field relation is evidence of that difference, not grounds to call F invalid. First capture is not selected just because it is smaller.

### C. Incumbent continuation / branch inheritance

- **Initiation/inclusion:** nominate one observed connection piece at START; follow its continuation rather than choosing every newly available branch. Exclude competitors by incumbency, not highest chemical/contact score.
- **Same materialization:** would require an explicit correspondence relation across structural movement, splits and reconnections. Current candidate similarity is not that relation.
- **Omission/branches:** alternative continuation stays outside unless assigned to the incumbent. A fork needs a declared inheritance rule, not a claim that all supported branches must materialize.
- **Termination:** close incumbent scope at existing COMMIT. Loss of correspondence must be represented as unresolved or absent scope; it must not silently restart the EVENT or alter commit scheduling.
- **Finiteness:** one retained continuation or bounded branching lineage. The correspondence/branch rule must bound membership; merely using the word lineage does not do so.
- **Existing information:** time-indexed selected/qualified graphs and endpoints. Motion/gait is neither necessary nor justified here.
- **New assumption/cost:** branch identity and inheritance under raster changes. No current persistent branch IDs exist. P is only an unbranched structural reference, not a validated implementation of C.

**Disposition:** potentially meaningful but under-specified. It is not a simpler current law. A geometry-proximity matcher would add ontology and threshold/convention issues; there is no justification to implement it merely to suppress valid omissions.

## 6. What current I can and cannot supply

**[New synthesis from implementation + observations]** I is more useful as a temporary materialization proxy than the earlier completeness framing allowed:

- **Finite and selective:** a non-exhaustive parent-route projection; not all valid alternatives.
- **Coherent:** one 8-connected structural snapshot at every sampled anchor; actual production descriptors already reach the existing compiler. No new scope/body/compiler qualification is claimed.
- **Reproducible:** frozen extraction and rerun agree; deterministic selector rules are visible.
- **Event-relative:** selected at a particular matured commit. This means temporal attribution, not guaranteed unique geometry or permanent physical branch identity.
- **Stable enough for its actual timer:** the saved clock reaches 20 s under the current ribbon/path continuity predicate. This is not stability of one fixed I topology, and the I turnover must remain disclosed.

Its limitations remain substantial: root comes from a privileged annulus, terminals from observation-edge reach, choice of graph parent is authored, and raster skeleton can change membership. It cannot yet be promoted as an open-world-compatible body selection law. Removing source/boundary alone discards the current responsibilities for root, scale, terminals and selectivity. That redesign is neither required nor attempted here.

The recommended contract therefore has two layers: **working scope semantics A** and **current I as its temporary deterministic realization**. Keeping this realization provisionally is a simplification, not adoption of a new mechanism or proof that the exact chosen paths are the world's natural material truth.
