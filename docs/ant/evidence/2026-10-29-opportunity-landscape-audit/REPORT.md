# What opportunities does the current environment provide?

## 1. Decision

**Partial characterization.** The observed early world is neither one isolated valid opportunity nor only interchangeable micro-noise. It contains **one persistent entry-region mechanical maximum, persistent weaker secondary regions, and a changing small-peak background**. Chemical memory has a flatter hierarchy among separated centers than D. Recent feet remain concentrated near entry too, so the evidence does not make entry bias a peculiarity of D or show that its maximum is simply abandoned old memory.

**[Architecture inference]** Max-D priority would consistently choose the entry neighborhood on these frozen states. That is an explicit preference for the strongest sampled mechanical conditioning, not an environment-mandated material origin. Other sustained qualified structures exist, including material-free ones. Allocation must eventually state why this history should get the episode, but this audit chooses no arbitration rule.

**[Boundary]** Qualified multi-channel dynamics are measured only for 0–120 s of one seed. Fifteen later stored checkpoints support a narrower, three-seed statement about **unconditioned mechanical context**; they do not establish the late qualified opportunity hierarchy. The next smallest pass should capture that missing late conditional landscape, not implement fairness, novelty, depletion, stochastic choice or a max-D replacement.

Labels throughout distinguish **[Implementation fact]**, **[Existing evidence]**, **[New diagnostic result]**, **[Architecture inference]**, **[Authored-law possibility]**, and **[Artistic interpretation]**. “Opportunity” is the question being investigated; measured peaks/groups/tracks are not natural material entities.

## 3. Diagnostic definitions

**[Implementation fact]** E requires B-memory ≥ .30 and sampled support D ≥ .015. F is current production thinning(E). S is edge-bearing F, the spatial domain of the current comparison nucleus. SurfaceField is foot-written, saturating normalized compaction with 1800 s default half-life; D is its center-weighted `.025` support readout. B is a bounded nonlinear pheromone projection with 20 s EMA, not raw pheromone mass. D/C/recent feet share causal input and are not three independent confirmations.

**[New diagnostic conventions]** On the same S, separately enumerate positive 8-neighbor maximum plateaus of D, B, C and a recent-foot summary. A plateau is exact-equal connected values; its canonical coordinate is the lowest index. Higher neighboring S values disqualify it. These are **S-restricted signal peaks**, affected by qualification, thinning, endpoints and raster connectivity.

Preserve all peaks. Two component-aware, strongest-first spatial grouping summaries use `.15` and `.30` world units. They are declared descriptive scales: X radius and twice that radius. Group membership is greedy and nontransitive, depends on canonical plateau points and order, and never merges different F components. Hence groups can be geographically close across disconnected components; group counts are **not counts of independent material opportunities**. All member lists, diameters and close cross-component pairs remain inspectable.

Recent feet are individually recorded contacts in `(t−20s,t]`. Exact-cell counts and `.037` neighborhood counts are separate; `.037=.025+.012` is a derived contact/readout envelope, not uncertainty or exclusive ownership. A foot can enter several neighborhoods. Twenty initial snapshots have partial temporal exposure; only nine of them have S. Full-window comparisons use the remaining 100 snapshots.

Temporal correspondence uses reciprocal nearest centers of the `.15` summaries, maximum displacement `.025`, sensitivity `.075`. These are matching conventions, not identity or UPDATE. Unmatched means no reciprocal match within cutoff, not environmental birth/death. Rank changes, pairwise rank-order inversion among continuing matches, and global-winner changes are recorded separately. Quantiles use the nearest observed order statistic, index `round((n−1)p)`; missing second/third ranks remain null.

## 9. Landscape regime and allocation responsibility

The most defensible description is **a persistent mechanically dominant entry region plus persistent weaker and short-lived secondary signal structure**. In the user's regimes:

- A is partly present as a clear D hierarchy, but not as “only one valid opportunity.”
- B is present as multiple persistent regions, with especially similar separated strengths in chemical memory. D's secondary values are usually substantially lower, so “several equal D opportunities” would overstate the result.
- C is a mixture, but secondary structure is not exclusively transient, and the primary is actively reinforced.
- D's **exclusive** bias claim is not supported; C and recent loading also favor entry. D's priority geometry/hierarchy differs from chemistry.
- E describes some micro-peak/count ambiguity, not the entire landscape. Long correspondences and geographically separated strong secondary regions exist.

**[Architecture inference]** The next allocation contract must distinguish **available qualified history** from **the priority assigned to it**. A maximum-D rule would be an authored commitment to strongest mechanical conditioning, often reproducing entry concentration. The world does not require that priority, but neither do these data require fairness, depletion, random selection or spatial novelty. Repeated same-place layers remain compatible with current ontology.

**[Authored-law possibilities, not recommendations]** Strongest-conditioned priority, recent-inscription priority or deliberately plural opportunity arbitration would express different material responsibilities. No such mechanism is implemented or selected here. Capture X remains unchanged; UPDATE is still a later responsibility and cannot be presumed to solve allocation meaning.

**[Artistic interpretation]** Whether a world repeatedly materializes its strongest conditioning or lets weaker persistent places also initiate episodes is a material-world choice. The present audit supplies its factual alternatives; it does not declare the visually distributed world superior.
