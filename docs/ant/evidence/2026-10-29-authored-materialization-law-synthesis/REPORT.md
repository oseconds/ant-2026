# One authored coherence scale for a finite material episode

## 1. Recommendation and remaining meaning choice

**[Authored material semantics]** A materialization episode may freeze a finite connected neighborhood of qualified structural history. Environmental history may extend far beyond it. The neighborhood has an explicitly authored coherence scale; valid omitted history need not be false, later recovered or eventually materialized.

**[Architecture synthesis]** This is a defensible source-neutral replacement *direction* for production's source→boundary authorship. It is not inferior merely because the scale is authored. It states more directly what the material episode is permitted to encompass, while retaining existing qualification, structural reduction and lossy selection.

Here “source-neutral” means **no designated source coordinate or boundary-target semantics in capture**. It does not mean source-independent inputs, unclipped world observation, or invariant tie selection on every field.

**Outcome B: retain two authored contracts, not a natural winner.**

- **X — ground-local connected capture** is the recommended **minimal geometric baseline for the next closed validation**: around an explicit history-conditioned nucleus, capture the nucleus-connected F structure inside a Euclidean disk of radius R. It needs a disk and connected-component operation, and does not let shortest-route detours determine the scope boundary.
- **G — connected-history coherence** is a genuine alternative: capture F within graph distance R from that same nucleus. It says coherence travels along the represented connection. This is a stronger material meaning and a stronger dependence on F's graph metric.
- **L — one bounded limb** is parked as the default. It is a valid authored projection, but reintroduces an endpoint winner and discards local branching. Its small extent alone is not a reason to prefer it, and the measured limb switches are a practical warning.

**[Unresolved artist/modeling decision]** One meaning choice remains: **does an episode cohere within a neighborhood of the ground, or over a bounded distance along the qualified connection?** Existing ANT evidence and `world-model.md` do not settle that. The preference for X is an economy/geometry argument, not proof of artistic identity or physical crystallization. Do not choose by asking which panel looks prettier.

No scale calibration, UPDATE law, material body, compiler, audio or production adoption follows. A clear shared architecture is available even though its metric remains an authored choice.
