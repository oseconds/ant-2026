## Definitions

The primary comparator remains exact equality on matched prior Plate ID and candidate source/target/path/mask identity, with at least one `CONTINUED` and one `RESTARTED` observation. No similarity fallback qualifies. Candidate-lineage transitions and per-cell eligibility transitions remain separate axes. A sampled local loss→return requires both sampled transitions inside the previous/current candidate-or-prior territory union. The observer uses the unchanged approximately one-second production analysis cadence; one-second sampling cannot establish subsecond absence.
