## Finding

The stored material history is richer than the normal view. The model preserves immutable Plate descriptors and their birth times, an ordered per-cell stack, top identity, layer count, and depth. Current locomotion, renderer, nodule qualification, and audio use different slices of that state. In particular, locomotion uses only the aggregate column count; the renderer hides most top identity and caps depth opacity; physical audio uses ordered Plate geometry and actual exposed-top routing; nodule formation uses the whole local column plus compaction and foot load.

A top-age map needs no added state. In an occupied cell, `topLayerByCell[cell]` is the index of the last Plate in that cell's append-only column. Its descriptor birth time is therefore the time that Plate became the current top. Given the controller snapshot's simulation clock:

```text
topIndex = snapshot.stratigraphy.stratigraphy.topLayerByCell[cell]
topBirthMs = snapshot.stratigraphy.plates[topIndex].bornAtSimulationTimeMs
topAgeMs = snapshot.analysis.simulationTimeMs - topBirthMs
```

This is exact time since the current top Plate arrived in the model. It is not time since a foot contact or evidence of wear. A continuously updated age color would keep changing after the material itself stops changing, so it should be treated as a diagnostic map at fixed simulation times, not as proof of ongoing material turnover.

## Causal ownership in the production source

| Path | Stored information that affects it | Information it does not use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plate qualification and birth** — [TrailPlateBirth.js](../../../../src/lib/agents/ant/TrailPlateBirth.js#L27) | One-second analysis of 20-second chemical memory and mechanical support; cumulative cell-level accepted-foot counts; current candidate continuity; prior `birthRecords` for same-corridor similarity, age, and new-contact refractory checks. The selector anchors source choice at `(0.5, 0.72)`, follows a deterministic weighted route to a distant observation-boundary target, and applies a 0.30 geodesic floor. | It does not query `MaterialStratigraphy`, prior material union, layer order, current top IDs, or buried-Plate identity. There is no prior-union novelty veto. A similar corridor can be inscribed again after its 90-second refractory and 30-additional-contact gates; that is the authored repeat rule, not evidence of a broken history store. |
| **Material store and exposure** — [MaterialStratigraphy.js](../../../../src/lib/agents/ant/MaterialStratigraphy.js#L39) | Each accepted descriptor is frozen and appended in birth order. Its mask appends that Plate's index to each covered cell's bottom-to-top column. A later overlap changes the top index, count, and depth; prior geometry remains immutable. No per-Plate removal or eviction path exists. | Age alone does not bury a Plate, and current material does not deform or resurface older geometry. Top turnover comes from later appended masks. |
| **Locomotion** — [AntController.js](../../../../src/lib/agents/ant/AntController.js#L330) | A tick-entry snapshot supplies only the current cell's aggregate `layerCountByCell` value `L`. Production multiplies the existing final desired speed by `m(L) = 1 - 0.10 * (1 - exp(-L / 4))`; contacts and births later in a tick apply from the next tick. | It does not use Plate identity, age, order, thickness, or depth to steer. More actual layers in a capacity shadow change `L`, and therefore movement; raising the cap is not storage-only after the original ceiling. |
| **Nodule qualification** — [MaterialStratigraphy.js](../../../../src/lib/agents/ant/MaterialStratigraphy.js#L91) | The ordered cell column must contain at least four Plates and three buried layers; the same cell also needs compaction of at least 0.55, local actual foot load of at least 200, and 30 seconds of continuous qualification. The resulting Nodule records source Plate IDs, bottom foundation ID, buried thickness/depth, and material derived from layer count, compaction, and load. There is a separate 32-Nodule ceiling. | A Plate count alone does not cause a Nodule. The source requires persistent mechanical and contact evidence as well as overlap. |
| **Normal renderer** — [AntRenderer.js](../../../../src/lib/renderers/AntRenderer.js#L221) | The frozen material image reads `topLayerByCell` and `layerCountByCell`. Baseline gray opacity depends on column count and clips at 21 alpha, reached at 10 or more layers. The optional `recent` view maps any top Plate among the newest eight to the same amber RGBA value; it does not distinguish identities or birth ages within that cohort. The debug Plate outlines can show per-Plate ID colors, but those controls are off by default. | The normal material image does not use per-cell Plate birth time, per-ID color, `plateHistory`, or `totalDepthByCellMeters`. Thus same-depth top-ID changes can leave baseline pixels unchanged, and changes within the latest-eight group can leave recent pixels unchanged. |
| **Physical audio** — [SurfaceAudioBridge.js](../../../../src/lib/audio/SurfaceAudioBridge.js#L78), [compileSurfaceTopology.js](../../../../src/lib/audio/compileSurfaceTopology.js#L19) | The compiler consumes ordered Plate descriptors, masks/material, Nodule descriptors, and stratigraphy order. It derives per-cell top layer and stack membership for routing and inter-layer links. Direct foot contacts route to the true full-world exposed top; the full layered topology supplies the existing Plate and Z mechanics. This path needs individual geometry and identity, not only `L`. | Audio does not use `plateHistory` age summaries or the per-cell `totalDepthByCellMeters` array as its mechanics input. The bridge and topology compiler each retain a 256-layer ceiling; any audio behavior beyond 256 material layers is unverified and would need a separate capacity/residency decision. |

Production default capacity is aligned at 256 in `TrailPlateBirth.maxBirths`, `MaterialStratigraphy.maxPlates`, and the audio bridge/topology. The two world-side gates are explicit: birth generation returns no descriptor after its birth ceiling, while `MaterialStratigraphy.addPlate` rejects at its ceiling. The material ceiling also validates the ordinary constructor range at 256. The 1,024 history-capacity run is therefore a harness-only shadow, not an ordinary production option. It allows real new Plates that subsequently change `L`, column-based Nodule eligibility, top identity, and renderer pixels. The shadow's extra post-256 evolution cannot be described as passive retention of already-existing data.

## What the long-run evidence establishes

The [long-run report](../2026-10-21-long-run-exhibition/REPORT.md) and [daily record](../../daily/2026-10-21-long-run-exhibition.md) distinguish accelerated model time from wall-clock operation. The default-cap visual run reached 256 Plates at 3,814.783 simulated seconds (63:34.783); after that, material union, top, depth, and turnover were static through 7,200 seconds while ants and pheromone continued. The accelerated 1,024-capacity shadow reached 474 Plates in 7,200 simulated seconds. From 15 to 120 minutes it added only 377 union cells despite 425 more Plates. Its final minute had three births, 2,531 top-identity cells changed at the sampled checkpoints, and zero union growth. Those figures show new layering over mostly occupied space; they do not decide whether its changing top partition is visually legible.

A concurrent metrics result supplied for this audit reports that the 3,600-to-7,200-second shadow interval added 35 union cells while 6,765 top-map entries differed; the shared descriptor prefix retained identical masks. Final integrity checking clarified that 6,730 changes are identity replacement on previously occupied cells and 35 are newly occupied entries. This audit did not recompute those numbers. Even if confirmed, top-ID change alone is not a boundary-shape metric: identity labels can change with little boundary displacement, while additions can also change the geometry of the top-identity partition. Compare the actual partition-edge sets (or a fixed-grid edge distance/overlap) at the two snapshots before calling it geometric evolution.

The artist found the latest-material preview still similar. That is consistent with two competing explanations: (1) substantial top-identity exchange is hidden by the gray/binary-cohort renderer, or (2) new masks mostly cover the same saturated planform and move few salient boundaries. Both fit the present evidence. The per-ID debug palette could expose identity exchange but may make relabeling look more morphologically distinct than it is. A monochrome boundary map of true adjacent top-ID changes answers the geometry question without giving each Plate an arbitrary color.

## Earlier selector evidence and limits

The [movement/material audit](../../daily/2026-10-08-movement-material-morphology.md) found the clearest observed shape filter in source/boundary eligibility plus skeleton pruning: roughly 71–73% of eligible skeleton cells were omitted; longest-component competition and minimum-geodesic rejection did not filter those runs. This is descriptive evidence from three 600-second seeds, not proof that the selector alone causes long-run saturation.

The preserved local-accretion shadow ref `ant-local-accretion-shadow` resolves locally to evidence commit `13ffc9f42bc32083c87c6b1c97bc82092e03e472`. Its evidence README reports broader early raster topology and lower repeated-cell freezing, followed by a single dense fan/blob-like component holding 99.43–99.72% of local material in all three seeds. It was a frozen-history shadow without new controller, renderer, stratigraphy, or audio behavior, and it was not adopted. This is a direct counterexample to assuming that removing path selection by itself sustains macro-form diversity. The contemporaneous decision is in [the daily record](../../daily/2026-10-08-local-accretion-shadow-decision.md).

The interior-endpoint shadow showed additional source-connected skeleton network, but the production-candidate branch `ant-interior-endpoint-production-candidate` resolves locally to evidence commit `bb970505eb63e9f69d2d00bc302ee72fbe19419e`. Across three matched 600-second seeds, the candidate increased Plate births and branch size while median prior-union overlap rose to 0.968–0.976 and median new area per birth fell to 0.00637–0.00920. The final union gain was only 2.5–5.1 percentage points. It is rejected for current adoption; do not restart the branch-richness experiment as if branches implied novelty. The shadow and rejection records are [here](../../daily/2026-10-08-interior-endpoint-shadow.md) and [here](../../daily/2026-10-08-interior-endpoint-production-rejection.md); the shadow evidence package is [here](../2026-10-08-interior-endpoint-shadow/README.md).

These negative results rule out treating local accretion or interior-endpoint enrichment as established solutions. They do not establish that all formation-history mechanisms fail. The separate temporal-episode path remains parked under the current investigation boundary; this audit does not reopen it. The current evidence does not identify whether entry geometry, source anchoring, renderer compression, or spatial saturation dominates.

## Lowest-cost next counterfactual

Before a world-law intervention, use the already captured shadow snapshots in one fixed-viewport, read-only renderer comparison:

1. Keep the same snapshot times, scale, material/pheromone layers, and camera. Compare the current renderer against a monochrome edge map of the **actual current top-Plate partition**. Add a top-birth-time or fixed birth-cohort readout only as a second diagnostic view; both are derivable from existing snapshots. Do not use a continuously changing age fade as evidence of turnover.
2. Report the union footprint, changed top-ID cells, and partition-edge change separately. This prevents color/label turnover from standing in for new area or boundary motion.
3. Ask whether the change is legible at the intended display scale. This is an artistic judgment, not a metric acceptance rule.

This counterfactual changes only the renderer's current gray/depth and single latest-eight color projection. It leaves source masks, birth/refractory rules, RNG law, locomotion feedback, material/audio physics, and stored history fixed. It is the cheapest test of whether existing history is being hidden by presentation. The prior latest-eight comparison was not judged successful, and the exact built-in still comparison in the long-run report was not run, so neither result settles this read-only alternative.

If the boundary map itself remains similar and the artist still cannot read the turnover, the next decision needs a named world-level objective before choosing a production cause. The fixed spawn distribution and selector share a source anchor: the spawn center is `(0.5,0.72)`, radius `0.018`, mean heading `−π/2` with `±0.3` spread; `TrailPlateBirth` uses the same center with source annulus radii `[0.055,0.115]`. This is a plausible constraint, not isolated causation. Any later source/entry test should change one factor per pass and account for spawn-placement retries changing shared RNG call counts. Do not combine it with selector, novelty, or material-law changes.

No production change is recommended from this audit. It establishes that top tenure is derivable, that capacity shadows alter world mechanics after the cap, and that current high ID turnover still needs a boundary-geometry and perceptual check before it supports a new formation mechanism.
