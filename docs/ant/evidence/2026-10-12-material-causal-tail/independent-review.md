# Independent final review

**Decision: PASS WITH LIMITATIONS.** The evidence supports **SUFFICIENT CURRENTLY** for the stated current-world question; keep temporary organism after-effect / vitality **PARKED**. **Blocking issues: none for this bounded decision.** Long-horizon mediator attribution remains incomplete and is not needed to show that existing state carries the one-tick consequence.

## Causal and state-attribution findings

The intervention is well matched to the question. Each of nine prospective age-gate cases starts from a separate untreated natural checkpoint. Selection uses the first eligible tick and lowest numeric ant ID, with no future-exit or outcome condition. Treatment changes one focal `accelerateToward` call by dividing its speed argument by tick-entry `m(L)`, aligns `targetSpeed`, calls the original once, and restores the method. Heading, intent and RNG remain aligned at the intervention boundary. This estimates the total consequence of one local resistance-removal tick in the default-On world; it does not estimate a continuous-Off episode.

Five of nine cases reach a jointly-live boundary with both center-cell layer counts at zero before retirement. In all five, the primary focal-tick data show a position difference above the descriptive `1e-5` cutoff before the first RNG divergence. This is an ordinary carried movement-state difference after the center-cell resistance input is inactive. The isolated common-input reference independently illustrates why: velocity differences decay quickly under common input while the integrated position offset remains. A trajectory difference alone does not imply stored organism memory.

All five observed exits later reenter material. The primary metrics count only jointly-live, center-cell `L=0` tick entries, but `L=0` does not rule out foot contact. Reentry may reactivate the local law. Last-above-cutoff timestamps therefore do not mean continuous persistence; the reported bouts correctly expose gaps. Four cases retire before a joint exit and are censored, so their post-exit response is unobserved rather than zero.

The sufficiency controls are credible for this narrow inference. Main-runner no-op controls match the untouched natural controller's audited full state at 600 s in 9/9 cases; complete-state restore is exact in 5/5 carrier probes. The separate unobserved replay reports exact matching for its specified counts, RNG, raw field hashes and agent JSON hashes across three seeds. It is not an independent full-V8-state hash comparison, and the review does not treat it as one.

The mobile-only splice yields a focal response in 5/5 cases, supporting sufficiency of the carried mobile block. That block bundles agent position/velocity with gait, pose, gaster, RNG and lifecycle state, so it does not quantify position-only mediation. The natural pre-RNG `L=0` measurements and isolated mechanical reference support the simpler kinematic carrier without requiring that inference from the splice alone.

Environmental evidence is secondary and weak. The world-only / pheromone-only restarts show small focal responses in three cases, below the descriptive position cutoff; two first responses occur while both center cells remain at `L=0`, and one reported onset is near numerical precision. One environmental restart fully restores its control world; two cases show no focal response. These results support a possible existing pheromone route, not a dominant or robust material-mediated tail.

Probe maxima need the boundary stated in `README.md`: they include all samples where both focal ants are live, including exposed intervals and reentries, rather than only joint-`L=0` samples. Probe reentry can reactivate the production law; pre-RNG maxima in probes are also not inherently `L=0`-conditioned. Do not use those maxima as clean post-exit measurements or as mediation fractions. The primary CSV-derived joint-`L=0`, pre-RNG values are the appropriate evidence for the focal tail.

Every 600 s descendant world differs, but those endpoints occur after focal paired comparisons are censored and include later shared-RNG divergence. They show durable consequences in the executable world, not continued experience in the original ant and not a uniquely identified pheromone, occupancy, collision or friction path. Contact/material, gait, field and RNG changes are existing world-state carriers; the production source has no dedicated material after-effect field, and no new organism state is required by this result.

## Decision boundary and limitations

The current evidence supports **SUFFICIENT CURRENTLY** because the intervention produces a reproducible, pre-RNG focal state difference during joint center-cell `L=0`, ordinary movement state can carry it, and the current executable world can retain downstream material/environmental differences. This is a modeling decision scoped to this world and this one-tick perturbation. It does not establish biological memory, perceptual or acoustic salience, a population effect, or the response to a full exposure episode. Three deterministic seeds and five uncensored joint exits are not a population estimate; prior layer duration was not randomized; the cutoffs are descriptive rather than perceptual; and no collision-only or longer-horizon attribution was performed.

A future organism after-effect would need a distinct responsibility: with the same complete current production state, environment and RNG, prior material exposure alone changes a specified response for a bounded duration and output. This experiment establishes no such residual dependence and selects no additional state, gain, steering rule or decay time.

Production-source hashes and checks were inspected; this review did not rerun the full simulation, tests or build. No archive sources were used, and no production files were changed.
