# Plate temporal-envelope and modulation protocol

This protocol is frozen before corpus analysis. It uses objective fixed-gain response PCM only. It contains no listener data or subjective fields.

## Inputs and receipts

Analyze the 36 natural native responses in the frozen 12-Plate roster at p25, p50 and p75, plus all six synthetic fixed-gain responses. Read natural stereo Float32 PCM from the existing ignored render outputs and synthetic stereo IEEE Float32 WAVs from their manifest-declared fixed-gain paths. Verify every byte count and SHA-256 against the respective render manifest before analysis. Verify the natural index/roster, event schedule, fixed gain, sample rate and frame count. Read only the fixed-gain source file named by each synthetic render-manifest case.

Source bytes are read-only. The analysis rechecks all source hashes before writing the result package.

## Envelope representation

Reuse `scripts/ant-base-born-loss-analysis.py` without modifying it. The transform is the existing 1024-sample symmetric Hann STFT at 48 kHz with a 256-sample hop, one-sided linear power normalized by the Hann sum, positive-bin power doubled, and 30 equal-ERB-rate bins over 0–8 kHz. Average left and right linear band power per frame. Preserve all 30 bands and list bands with no FFT-bin centers; do not fill empty bands.

The source frame timestamp is the left edge `n*256/48000`. Envelope features use the Hann-window center, `n*256/48000 + 511.5/48000`. There are 279 STFT frames. Frame 278 begins at 1.482667 s, contains 832 source samples and 192 zero-padded samples, and is excluded from decay, modulation, t90 and tail/early metrics. The first 278 frames have full source support. To verify compatibility with the existing ERB analysis, its full-time, all-frame integrated and left-edge late-tail profiles deliberately retain all 279 stored frames, including frame 278.

For the compact envelope coordinate, sum linear power over fixed ERB band ranges `[0,10)`, `[10,20)` and `[20,30)` to form low, mid and high groups. Convert group power to dB only after summing. Keep each group’s slope and residual RMS.

## Event, decay and modulation

The scheduled contact begins at source frame 480, or 10 ms, and lasts 3 ms, ending at 13 ms. Fixed-gain event/level measurements come directly from PCM: peak, full-clip RMS, pre-event RMS, contact-window RMS, and early post-contact RMS windows. Also retain level-invariant attack companions: peak-to-full-RMS crest in dB, and the 13–50 ms and 10–13 ms RMS ratios relative to full-clip RMS, expressed as dB differences. These ratios are unchanged by a common positive gain and do not replace the fixed-gain measurements. STFT windows starting before 13 ms are ineligible for post-pulse decay/modulation features. Fit and modulation features use complete frames whose center times fall in the fixed 50–500 ms interval; this yields 84 frames for the frozen recipe.

For each group, fit ordinary least squares to `10*log10(group power)` over those 84 centers. The slope in dB/s is a descriptive decay-envelope summary, not a free-pole or physical decay estimate. Subtract the fitted line and then remove the residual mean per group. The residual can contain envelope curvature, modal beating and other response changes; it is not interpreted as beating alone.

For each group, use a symmetric Hann over the 84 residual samples and compute the real FFT periodogram. Define each group weight as its share of mean linear group power over the same 84 frames. Form the pooled periodogram as the sum of the three group periodograms multiplied by these weights. This energy weighting prevents weak sideband leakage in an otherwise near-empty group from dominating its log residual; it also means the primary coordinate remains coupled to coarse ERB-group power balance. Preserve the three weights and each group’s unweighted residual RMS and selected-bin periodogram so changes in group responsibility remain inspectable. The pooled residual RMS is the square root of the weighted mean of the three residual mean-square values. Restrict the pooled periodogram to the empirically calibrated contiguous range, then unit-sum those selected bins. This is the primary modulation-rate shape. Its pair metric is cosine similarity, which is scale-free and phase-free. If total group energy is zero, the pooled residual RMS is at or below the calibrated no-AM floor, or the selected band contains less than 1% of total non-DC pooled periodogram power, mark the modulation spectrum unavailable.

The 84-frame DFT spacing is `187.5/84 = 2.232142857 Hz`; bin 1 is not a resolved 2 Hz periodicity. The primary lower candidate is bin 3 (three cycles across the window, 6.696428571 Hz). Bins 1 and 2 are reported only as trend-scale sensitivity checks. The envelope sampling rate is 187.5 frames/s and its Nyquist frequency is 93.75 Hz. That Nyquist value is not treated as usable bandwidth: the 21.333 ms Hann STFT window smooths envelope variation. The primary upper edge is frozen by same-pipeline known-AM probes across coherent 187.5/937.5/4125 Hz low/mid/high carrier bins and two phases: the highest contiguous tested candidate at or below 40 Hz for which every probe recovers at least half the expected fundamental depth, the pooled-spectrum peak error is no more than one modulation bin, and at least 1% of non-DC pooled periodogram power lies in the selected band. The pre-corpus validation selected bins 3–17, or 6.696–37.946 Hz; the next bin is 40.179 Hz and exceeds the 40 Hz candidate ceiling. The selected-band share also rejects normalized shapes dominated by out-of-band leakage. Also report the exact Hann-squared window transfer and higher-rate probes as sensitivity checks. These numerical AM gates describe estimator fidelity; they are not perceptual acceptance thresholds.

The full-clip companions use the 278 complete STFT frames: (1) t90 of normalized pooled 30-band frame power, measured from file start; (2) the dB ratio of summed frame power at centers `>=500 ms` to centers `50–500 ms`. These are overlapping-frame summaries, not physical decay constants or natural termination. Raw attack/level remains separate at fixed gain.

## Validation and comparison

Before corpus output, validate identical-input cosine, exact deterministic repeat, positive gain scaling invariance of detrended residuals, group weights, normalized selected spectrum and level-invariant attack ratios alongside the predicted raw RMS change, an exponential decay-only carrier, silence handling, known AM rates across all three carrier groups and phases, and a shared-rate multiband AM control. Require bin 3 to pass the AM fidelity gates and selected-band share; require the higher out-of-band probes at bins 21, 24, 32 and 42 to fail the selected-band-share gate. The exact-pipeline constant-carrier probes establish a maximum energy-weighted no-AM residual RMS of approximately 1.31e-13 dB; adding the declared 0.001 dB margin yields the calibration floor. The usable modulation range is based only on these synthetic validation probes, not the response corpus.

Write all 861 pair rows among the 42 responses. Label the natural 36 same-Plate cross-position pairs and 198 different-Plate same-position pairs; label all other natural, natural/synthetic and synthetic/synthetic combinations exploratory. Summarize only the defined 36/198 natural comparison for modulation shape, residual depth, group decay slopes and full-clip companions. Recompute the existing full-time, all-frame integrated and left-edge late-tail ERB profile cosines from the same fixed-gain PCM and verify all 234 previously reported natural pair values and within/between medians for each metric. No inferential p-values, subjective joins or listener fields are produced.

## Limits

The 30 bins are an STFT ERB-rate grouping, not 30 independent auditory channels. Modulation spectra discard phase and order, so a high cosine does not establish aligned envelope timing. The pair sets share source Plates and are descriptive dependent comparisons. The six synthetic cases are engineering controls; their cross-class pair rows are not pooled into natural summaries.
