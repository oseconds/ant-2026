## Result

The single local-accretion family removed source/boundary/path selection and directly matured locally supported cells from frozen natural histories.

It showed **mixed viability**:

- broader early raster topology,
- lower repeated-cell freezing than production,
- real digital holes and disconnected patches under the documented raster topology,
- but late convergence toward one dense fan/blob-like component in all three seeds.

At the final observation, the largest component contained 99.43–99.72% of local material. Most remaining holes and isolated components were tiny. More than half of frozen-cell events still re-inscribed previously occupied territory.

## Decision

Do not adopt this rule into production.

Preserve it as the comparison baseline for the claim:

> path selection is not the only source of morphology limitation; direct permanent translation of absolute accumulated state can itself converge toward late filling.

The next material hypothesis should be based on **history transition / coherent episodes**, not another parameter tune of this absolute local-accretion family.
