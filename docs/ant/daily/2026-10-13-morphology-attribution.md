## Decision

Movement is already locally radial (median tortuosity 1.18–1.22; radial alignment 0.90), but occupancy stays broadly distributed. Pheromone angular concentration shifted by seed, so its independent causal contribution remains unresolved. Mechanical support rejected almost no chemically qualified cells. The strongest observed compression is the source-annulus-to-boundary selector: it retained 29–32% of the chosen skeleton-component pixels before the frozen ribbon expands the area again.

Decision: **D primary as the observed funnel compression; A secondary upstream contributor; B mixed/unresolved; C secondary for chemical area and unsupported for mechanical filtering as the primary bottleneck; E multiple stages contribute.** Keep production unchanged. The smallest justified next pass is a read-only, selector-only shadow comparison on this baseline; it should not tune or replace production behavior.

Limits: natural stage relationships are not independent causal effects. Movement and pheromone share history, and these three seeds support bounded attribution only.
