## Limitations and next decision

This is an identity/data-stream intervention, not a physically simulated trajectory. Physical recruitment/exit geography, density and collision geometry are exact; label-specific birth/body alignment and identity motion are discontinuous. Scheduled old/new body separation averages about 0.40 arena units, and lifecycle repair distances about 0.57–0.58; those are logged remap distances, not speeds. Raw pair keys can persist through a body exchange without a new encounter, so null durations describe label streams rather than uninterrupted physical partner contact.

Event payloads expose limited actors. Actor-associated duration flags cannot prove full graph support was unaffected. Rolling cohorts overlap; removing a flag does not reset observer state or create a corrected null. The longer memory windows remain entangled with remaps and turnover.

**Decision:** S2b demonstrates sensitivity of temporal bookkeeping to this identity-remapping process beyond the instantaneous spatial movie, with most simple recent-graph differences concentrated around intentional identity breaks. It does not isolate a natural partner-continuity effect from artificial boundaries and turnover. It supports a metric-robustness review, not social preference, memory, roles, pioneers or organization. ANT identity also bundles inertia, personality, hesitation, pheromone adherence/history, occupancy interactions and path dependence.

Any further comparator or diagnostic requires a separately authorized pass. No new null family, spectral analysis, adaptive social memory, feedback, graph→Plate coupling, audio/runtime or substrate work is started. Artistic interpretation stays separate. Stop after the S2b push.
