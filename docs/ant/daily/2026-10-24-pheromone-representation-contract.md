## Investigation and new probe

The parent read current field, sensing, material and renderer consumers and the latest CIC qualification, stage attribution, transverse-profile and sample-substitution reports. Two read-only Luna subagents audited geometry (Max, because the uniform feasibility bound needs a proof) and consumer/unit coupling (High, because raw snapshots and scalar readers cross system boundaries). Neither changed files or ran a candidate.

A probe scope was written before executing a small deterministic geometry harness. It checked three isolated production writes, a production read beyond the brush radius, a cap accounting example, derived threshold witnesses and 10,201 triangle certificates. It did not implement a new representation or run the CIC cue chain.

The decisive result was structural: `2R < h` on the current grid, so a radius-limited disk can contain at most one node. Off-node exact centroids are impossible with nonnegative increments. The general all-position centroid feasibility threshold is N=250 at fixed R; four-corner CIC writer support requires N=316, while its complete bilinear readout support requires N=630. These are different contracts, not performance-selected resolutions.

The audit also found that `TrailPlateBirth` prefers raw arrays over `.sample()`. A density conversion only inside `.sample()` would split movement and material signal semantics. Exact node-sum conservation on a refined grid does not preserve the physical integral of the existing bilinear signal. Cap and domain-edge conditions require explicit exceptions or changed semantics. A perpendicular short-pair counterexample shows that dose/centroid conservation alone does not guarantee subcell direction preservation.
