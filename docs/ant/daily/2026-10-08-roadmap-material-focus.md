# 2026-10-08 — Roadmap refocus: material history

## Why this direction changed

S0/S1 established the read-only temporal proximity layer. S2a then showed that a simple circular synchrony null was strongly confounded by seams, recruitment phase, density and collision structure. S2b preserved the exact spatial movie and found that most recent-topology differences were concentrated around artificial identity remaps and lifecycle repairs. S2c finally tested whether selected causal temporal graph summaries added held-out predictive value beyond a compact current-frame spatial/movement baseline for future 1 s component merges.

S2c produced no consistent added-value signal across the three natural seeds and the current research decision is **spectral SKIP**. This does not prove that all temporal information is redundant and does not disprove social organization in general. It does mean that the present ANT implementation does not justify promoting the social observation layer into an explanatory or causal production mechanism.

## Current interpretation boundary

The social layer is not deleted.

It is preserved as a **frozen no-relationship diagnostic baseline**:

```text

```

If a future hypothesis explicitly adds relationship, partner memory, social preference or another social mechanism, that mechanism must be compared against this frozen baseline under matched seeds, movement conditions and observer definitions before any additional social-effect claim is made.

No λ₂/eigenmode, adaptive social memory, social feedback or graph→Plate coupling follows automatically from the current evidence.
