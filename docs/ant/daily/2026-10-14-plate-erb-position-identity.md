# 2026-10-14 — Plate ERB identity across contact position

Question: do frozen natural Plates retain similar pooled stereo ERB-time responses across p25/p50/p75, relative to different Plates at the same position?

Reused only the 36 native isolated one-shot PCM files from the existing sonic-individuality archive. Verified every path, byte count, and SHA-256 against its frozen render manifest before analysis. No simulation, contact, Plate, or physics render was added; no production or audio parameter changed.

Decision: **MIXED**. Full ERB-time shape does not show a same-Plate advantage over the same-position between-Plate control. Time-integrated and late-tail band profiles do, but vary across Plates and do not preserve the full temporal pattern. The matrix has partial, not consistent, Plate blocks. This is measured response structure only; no human listening assessment or identification claim.

The complete result, source receipts, pair CSV, validation, and two fixed-scale PNGs are in the [evidence package](../evidence/2026-10-14-plate-erb-position-identity/README.md). Production `src/` and `static/` remain unchanged.
