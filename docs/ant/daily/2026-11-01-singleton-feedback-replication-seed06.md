# 2026-11-01 — Singleton-only feedback replication, seed 20261006

## Question and scope

Did the exact singleton-only retained-material feedback overlay from the seed-20261005 pass reorganize B/C/X formation opportunities on a second matched 300 s production seed?

This was one CONTROL/TREATMENT pair on frozen authority seed `20261006`, 60 Hz, Audio Off. No observer or feedback law was changed, and no actual research Plate was made.

## Work

- CONTROL matched all 300 frozen E/F/B/C/D, production layer/top, birth, and RNG boundaries; the live observer matched 25,857 opportunity rows, 1,035,269 X memberships, and 325 maturation events.
- TREATMENT added its first singleton overlay after 39.0167 s; the first ant read it at 68.5833 s. Tick histories matched exactly through tick 4,115 and diverged at the first exposed tick.
- The overlay produced 161 singleton episodes (49 B, 38 C, 74 coeval `{B,C}`) and left 57 contested groups inert. It exposed 274 ants on 31,730/258,943 ant-ticks; speed and A changed on all exposed ticks and D on 1,624.
- Mean mature X options fell 10.43→8.24, contested share fell 38.62%→29.68%, B/C maturations fell 325→292, and unchanged production Plate births rose 7→9. The five windows varied, including a late increase in contested-option mean.
- Compared with seed 20261005, the aggregate directions reversed. Classification: **SEED-DEPENDENT FEEDBACK SENSITIVITY**.

## Decision

Direct feedback sensitivity occurred in seed 20261006, but it did not reproduce the aggregate direction from seed 20261005. This two-seed result shows seed-dependent response, not a universal law and not singleton formation validity.

Smallest next question: should another matched seed be authorized to characterize response direction before any actual-Plate integration?
