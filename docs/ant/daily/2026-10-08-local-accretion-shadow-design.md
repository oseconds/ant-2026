# 2026-10-08 — Local-accretion shadow morphology design

## Purpose

Test whether ANT material morphology becomes richer and more historically legible when frozen material is derived from **local accumulated evidence** rather than a selected source→boundary path/tree.

This is a shadow research pass only. Production `TrailPlateBirth`, movement, behavior, audio and stratigraphy remain unchanged.

## Starting evidence

The rejected interior-endpoint production candidate showed that adding more retained tree branches increases branch richness and Plate count, but also increases prior-union overlap and decreases median new area per birth. The current working interpretation is that the selector is becoming denser inside already represented territory rather than expanding the morphology vocabulary.

## Shadow hypothesis

A candidate material field should be derived locally from existing recorded evidence:

```text
persistent chemical evidence
×
mechanical / FOOT_CONTACT support
×
temporal persistence
→
local materialization state
```

Do not select a target path first. Do not require the resulting morphology to be a tree.

The mechanism may therefore admit, if supported by the archived fields:

- arterial corridors
- fans / deltas
- braided or reconnecting corridors
- cycles / loops
- isolated fossil patches
- palimpsest-like overlap

These are morphology classes to observe, not shapes to force.

## Frozen inputs

Reuse the existing committed 600 s natural archives for seeds:

- 20261005
- 20261006
- 20261007

Do not rerun or retune ANT behavior.

Reuse only already-recorded chemical / mechanical / contact evidence that can be reconstructed deterministically from the archives and existing replay tooling.

## First-pass constraint

Keep the mechanism intentionally small.

Prefer one scalar local materialization field plus one deterministic freeze rule. Reuse existing thresholds / persistence concepts where possible rather than inventing a large new parameter family.

Do not add:
- source→boundary target selection
- Dijkstra path selection
- random branch creation
- morphology templates
- audio coupling
- production Plate birth
- stratigraphy mutation
- behavior feedback

## Primary observables

Compare the shadow material output against current production history using fixed-scale measurements:

- connected-component count and area
- total occupied area
- new area over time
- overlap with previously frozen material
- skeleton branch / endpoint proxies
- cycle count or cycle-rank proxy
- isolated component / fossil count
- current-traffic vs frozen-history spatial mismatch
- morphology diversity across the three seeds

A cycle metric must describe actual closed connectivity in the shadow material; do not infer loops from visual appearance alone.

## Interpretation

Research result:
- whether local accumulated history can generate a broader morphology vocabulary while preserving deterministic dependence on recorded movement/environmental evidence.

Artistic interpretation:
- whether artery / fan / braid / fossil / palimpsest classes are visually compelling.

Do not convert artistic preference into a biological claim or numeric research threshold.

## Stop condition

Stop after one shadow local-accretion family and one three-seed 600 s comparison.

Do not adopt it into `TrailPlateBirth` automatically.

If the result only creates blobs/noise or saturates the arena, record that negative result and stop. If morphology broadens without immediate saturation, report it as a candidate for a later, separate production-design pass.
