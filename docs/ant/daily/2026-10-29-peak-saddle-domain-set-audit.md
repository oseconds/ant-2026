## Work and result

- Kept all finite lobes without size, prominence, rank or appearance filters. Recorded exact size distributions, total memberships, unique spatial coverage, all nonzero same-state overlaps, elder hierarchy, structural overlaps and two existing correspondence relations.
- Across 1,665 finite-domain observations, median size was 2 cells and 61.7% were 1–2 cells. Membership sums exceeded unique coverage (6,495 versus 4,720), and all 800 same-state overlaps were strict containment pairs.
- Multi-anchor correspondence families and a larger tail existed, but even five-anchor families had median size 2; size and contrast distributions did not separate a distinct class. Split/merge relations were common. Production-I overlap was limited; F/F\I overlap is contextual and E-derived, not independent validation.
- Independent review caught that 389 families had multiple split/merge markers, not necessarily incidents on multiple transitions. The receipt now counts distinct transitions: 300 span multiple transitions, while 89 of the multi-marker families are confined to one transition.
- Boundary deviation: one initial validation call to the parent combined `--check` recomputed its frozen 4/8 census. No 4-neighbor results were used in this interpretation; after recognizing the boundary, the remaining audit and independent review used the 8-only receipts.
- Decision: **`DOMAIN AVAILABILITY EXISTS BUT IS TOO UNDERDETERMINED`**. Finite spatial opportunities exist before UPDATE; no rule for choosing one was established. Peak–saddle availability remains research-only.

## Interpretation boundary

Authored semantics: 8-neighbor connectivity is the selected raster convention, not a physical law. No artistic/material mapping follows from this result.
