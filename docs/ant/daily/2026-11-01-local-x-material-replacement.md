# Local-X material replacement — bounded 300 s pass

## Question

Can accepted uncontested local singleton X episodes replace the authored source→boundary `TrailPlateBirth` selector as the only material birth source while preserving a stable, causally active ANT world?

## Baseline and protocol

- Experiment branch: `codex/ant-local-x-replacement-20261101`, based on `7c96c128bd32bea33f69d8a1c1d08cf85d4421f8`; production authority: `main@bc045dc3caa5954df633ecbee37fbabcb8224f30`.
- Three fresh CONTROL/REPLACEMENT pairs: seeds `20261005`, `20261006`, `20261007`; 300 s, 60 Hz, Audio Off.
- All CONTROLs matched 300 frozen native boundaries and source-to-boundary birth receipts. Their local B/C/X observer rows, captures and maturity streams also matched after one-to-one frozen/live track mapping.
- REPLACEMENT left native movement, chemistry, SurfaceField, selector and feedback active. Native selector descriptors were shadow-only; 436 accepted singleton-X Plates were the only replacement material births. Uncontested coeval same-X B/C episodes became one Plate; contested events remained observations.
- No production source change, tuning, Audio On, capacity increase, extended run, or Residence work.

## Results

- Every seed retained material throughout the horizon and completed without hitting the 256-Plate or native 32-Nodule ceiling. Final local Plates: 156, 143, 137. Their masks touched neither the entry zone nor authored boundary band in 114/156, 93/143, and 104/137 cases.
- Native material exposure was 61,087, 22,888 and 28,911 ant-ticks. Local material changed native speed/turn-rate multipliers; same-flow headway opportunities were also observed. Population stayed active with 14, 18 and 19 ants at the end.
- CONTROL instead ended with 14, 7 and 11 source-to-boundary Plates, 4,826 / 3,800 / 4,267 covered cells and 5 / 3 / 5 Nodules. REPLACEMENT ended with 2,218 / 1,990 / 2,128 covered cells and no Nodules. CONTROL material was one 8-connected body in each seed; local replacement material formed 53 / 44 / 39 components. Thus local material persisted and fed back, but coverage, connectivity and Nodule history entered a substantially different regime.
- The unchanged production selector emitted 12 / 12 / 14 shadow descriptors in the altered worlds. Their comparison with local actual births is descriptive only and selected no winner.

## Decision

**REPLACEMENT VIABLE BUT REGIME DISTINCT.** Local X is a credible production replacement candidate for artist review. This result is not an adoption decision. The clear spatial separation shows that retained material can arise without every episode connecting the authored entry region to the world boundary; it does not make boundary contact undesirable.

## Limits and next gate

The decision is limited to these three seeds and 300 s, Audio Off, singleton-only acceptance, the fixed neutral structural coefficients, and current native feedback/Nodule behavior. It does not establish an ontology, a permanent rule for contested events, B/C material character, Audio-On performance, long-term capacity/turnover, or production adoption. The next decision is artist review / production-candidate adoption; no automatic 600 s extension or further selector experiment follows.
