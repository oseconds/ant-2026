## Why preserve the raw outputs

These explorations are retained as process history because they expose design-space changes that may matter later even where individual claims are wrong or later rejected. In particular, Pass 2 explicitly revises parts of Pass 1 rather than replacing it, so preserving both makes the evolution of the reasoning inspectable.

Do not promote an idea from these files directly into current ANT planning without rechecking it against current source, current evidence, and subsequent dated research.
