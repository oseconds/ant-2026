# W1 Endogenous Formation — Contribution & Spatial Extent Design

**Date:** 2026-10-11 (Asia/Seoul)

**Decision:** **Undecided / Research Gap — no endogenous formation law selected.** Plate-native generation is operationally possible without an independent Material field, but the evidence does not select its contribution, spatial extent or repeat law. Existing Plate/Z retention-response is retained; Deposit remains derived.

**Integrated finding:** creation of a physical layer requires a declared conversion/configuration commitment and a declared layer identity. Neither a fresh contact, a field increment, an activity component nor a spatial union determines those commitments by itself. Spatial grouping becomes part of the physical law when it determines Plate node sets, in-layer connections or Z relations. Source already establishes that partition consequence; repeating it is not the next formation discriminator. A conditional conformance specification below is executable only after an explicit authored law choice, which this pass does not make.

[Process, delegation and verification](daily/2026-10-11-w1-endogenous-formation-contribution-spatial-extent.md). No new formation diagnostic, fixture, replay or evidence package is produced in this design pass.

These responsibilities can live in existing objects. A new predictive state remains new state even if hidden inside a Plate; a derived label remains a label even if put in a new class.

### What an existing field increment actually means

For a cell inside a valid contact's production kernel, the real-number update before Float32 storage is `Delta S = (1-S_pre) * contactDose * weight`, followed separately by clock-driven relaxation. The default dose is `.025`, radius `.012`, on 96² cells. A small-radius fallback writes the nearest cell. Strength and event centroid are ignored; each valid leg point is processed separately. `totalMass` in both field snapshots is a grid-value sum, not physical material mass.

At equal count, different pre-state or kernel weights give different increments. At `S=1`, a contact produces no increment; at `S=0` and weight 1 it adds `.025`. This is an analytical consequence of existing source, not a new experiment. Conversely, a contact can be defined by a future authored law to create a new configuration even at field saturation. That would be a configuration-generation assumption, not proof that dose measures mass. Neither choice is mandated.

Chemical deposition and mechanical dose have different origins, supports and clocks. Chemical input is distance/gaster based; its default field half-life is 15 s. Surface relaxation defaults to 1,800 s. Birth's bounded chemical EMA has a 20 s time constant sampled at roughly 1 s intervals. None of these times is a measured conversion timescale. Repeated occupation can preserve memory or supply a later trigger; it need not deposit a new layer every time. A delayed process could also form after activity stops if its progress/availability law is explicitly declared. Fresh contact is not a universal necessary condition.

### Time alignment and vertical attribution are missing inputs

[AntController.tick](../../src/lib/agents/ant/AntController.js) relaxes/decays once, then processes agents, gaster deposits and contacts in loop order; contact handling precedes birth analysis and stack insertion. The emitted touchdown time can lie inside that tick. A later field snapshot is therefore not an event-time pre/post pair. A proposed marginal contribution needs a stated source-processing order or an explicitly different time integrator; sorting timestamps and treating it as production replay would change the contract.

The fields and contact counters are shared XY states. `TrailPlateBirth.advance` receives pheromone, surface and time, not current stacks/exposure. The same XY activity cannot thereby be assigned to a particular exposed/buried layer. [Stratigraphy contact load](../../src/lib/agents/ant/MaterialStratigraphy.js) is also an XY count; exposed-only audio routing is a different responsibility. A new law must say whether activity creates a layer above the present top, changes an existing configuration, or causes no retained change. It cannot infer that choice from audio target routing.

Existing nodule creation already demonstrates another authored local world mechanism using overlap, compaction, contact load and persistence. It is not evidence of a general material-conversion law or a reason to add Deposit identity; this pass preserves it.

## 3. Spatial extent: qualification, organization and compilation

Production's supported mask is joint qualification on a 96² grid: chemical EMA at least `.30`; only then sample support within radius `.025`, requiring at least `.015`. Conditional unsampled support zero is not measured disappearance of material. `selectCandidate` then skeletonizes, finds 8-neighbor components, requires the fixed annulus around `(0.5,.72)` with radii `.055–.115` and an observation-edge target within `.05`, and chooses weighted shortest-path networks from one annular seed. Routes must reach geodesic length `.30`; only the best eligible component is retained. The descriptor mask is a ribbon with half-widths derived from the broader supported component and clamped to `.012–.030`.

Thus the registered extent is neither the raw contact kernel nor the complete qualified field. Source annulus, endpoint restrictions, component competition, thinning, width clamp and ribbon rasterization are model conventions. They can serve an authored formation model, but a change in any of them can change births without identifying a new local conversion. The annulus is not observed material supply. Relocating the same local history away from the annulus or edge can suppress registration; releasing that restriction alone does not define another law.

| Spatial question | Required commitment | Counterexample / existing limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local reach | Define which input or interaction can change which locations; distinguish input kernel, eligibility neighborhood and formed footprint | Neighbor dose can affect a cell with no point contact; support radius is larger than contact radius |
| Temporal reach | Define formation progress, retained effects and whether old memory remains eligible; record its actual clock | Historical union includes inactive cells; long transitive chains need not form a simultaneous body |
| Multi-individual history | Specify superposition, interaction, or reaction; individual identities may be provenance only | Two identical point histories with different ant labels have the same SurfaceField operator; requiring different ants would be a new law |
| Growth / split | Specify whether accepted material grows, increments accumulate before birth, or separate events create separate layers | Episode split/closure changes output identity without an independently identified conversion; immutable Plate growth would need a new representation/transition rule |
| Repeat / overlap | Decide new top layer, in-place change, shared effect, or no change | Same corridor can match by tolerant path with low mask IoU; reusing a union is not a new increment |
| Grid / sampler / compiler | Pin source cells, sampling clock, solver projection and any connectivity law; test aliasing before scale claims | 96² source cells project conservatively to 32² nodes; close disconnected cells can occupy adjacent/same solver cells; source edges can add diagonal bridge nodes |

Singletons can result from an explicitly local unit law or sampled termination grouping. Broad connected regions can result from cumulative support, irreversible retention or actual retained relations. Explain the mechanism first; neither outcome authorizes dilation, size vetoes, chain cuts, morphology thresholds or a desired visual target.

### A structural counterexample: equal union is not equal physical world

This is a source-derived analytical counterexample, not an executed fixture. Put source cells `(row=48,x=32)` and `(row=48,x=33)` in one Plate, with empty edges and valid pickups. They map to adjacent solver cells `(row=16,x=10)` and `(row=16,x=11)` and have an in-layer neighbor connection. Represent the same binary union as two one-cell Plates: there is no cross-Plate in-layer connection and no source-cell overlap for a Z link. Two identical-mask Plates instead have separate node sets and an overlap link where one union Plate has one node set.

The compiler therefore makes layer partition a physical commitment. A batch, component ID or export boundary cannot silently determine the number of material bodies while being described as representation only. This is compatible with derived Deposit: grouping already fixed Plates for a query is different from merging their physical degrees of freedom. Coarse-grid coincidence also does not prove source-level cohesion; document its numerical effect as a compilation convention.

## 4. Repeated deposition and retained consequence

Production carries candidate persistence across mask/path similarity at least `.6`, chooses the newest qualifying prior birth, and gates repeats with 90 s refractory plus 30 additional points in the **prior frozen ribbon**. The global gap is 10 s. Birth does not consume chemical EMA, SurfaceField or old contact counts. The public descriptor's contact metric uses the larger supported component, so it cannot replace the private ribbon counter.

The [locality audit](evidence/2026-10-11-w1-repeat-birth-contact-locality-audit/README.md) found five repeats among 91 births; all new masks also contain at least 30 post-baseline points. Their mask IoU is about `.172–.222`; all matches use tolerant principal paths. Shared interval keys show observational reuse, not an exclusive material budget. This supports those five footprints' contact locality only.

A future law may allow new activity at the same place to create a new layer, deform existing material, replenish a precursor, or do nothing retained. Old memory can participate nonexclusively in multiple events if declared. Rejecting a duplicate event key is a transaction/idempotence rule; it is not conservation. A conserved amount would additionally need units, supply, transfer/loss and balance. Recovery and finite precursor are optional new assumptions, not facts hidden in field saturation or a technical Plate cap.

For Plate-native creation, the retained change can be one immutable configuration and its ordered stack/node/link increment. It need not add a constitutive field or intrinsic Character. Later exposure, depth and vibration remain current conditions; age does not cause burial. The completed attribution explains one supplied-layer consequence using existing state. It must not be counted again as evidence that an additional Material variable is necessary.

Mechanical conversion would require compatible loading/control, units, initial/reference configuration and work boundary. Current strength/dose are not measured force/mass/deformation; `p` remains standalone mathematics. Live insertion work and awake/quiet-state transition remain unresolved. The present design does not install a new body into a live solver or change production clocks.

## 5. What is specifiable, and why no candidate is selected

An **unselected constructive countermodel** demonstrates that Plate-native endogenous generation is specifiable: use a pinned pre-event checkpoint of chemical EMA and support; a unique valid foot point qualifies under declared predicates; create one virtual local configuration at that point, store its provenance in a new immutable Plate, and append above the local old stack. No external configuration packet or extra Material field is logically required. This is not PN-arrival-0.

For a completely restricted example, declare nearest 96-grid cell as the entire footprint; `.30/.015` as activation predicates; fixed `.9/1.1/.35` coefficients and `.001` thickness; one accepted event key per Plate, regardless of observer batching; no growth/merge/erosion; a duplicate key has no additional effect while a distinct qualified key at the same place adds another layer. Define the key from immutable source-event identity plus foot ordinal/leg ID, never a delivery/batch number; conflicting payloads under one key are invalid input. Checkpoints explicitly carry source-processing boundary and sampler clocks, not inferred sub-tick fields. Descriptor provenance can reconstruct the accepted-key set; its one-use check is transaction identity, not material debit. Configuration persists after agents leave; capacity/invalid input stops the detached test rather than evicting history. Physical assembly/probing is restricted to rest; this does not close a live ANT transition.

Here "one virtual configuration per qualifying event," cell-scale extent, repeat multiplicity, top-layer placement and primitive mechanics are **authored assumptions**. A singleton is prescribed by this length/identity choice, not discovered. Count is not mass. Saturated fields can still permit repeated units because this example treats contact as trigger rather than material supply. It might hit the technical cap rapidly; that is a recorded model consequence, not an ecological saturation law or a reason to tune it. It supplies no history-derived intrinsic Character. The absence of an external packet makes the algorithm endogenous in the computational sense, not physically validated.

This countermodel is not chosen as ANT's priority formation law: current evidence supports neither one-event/one-cell units nor another specific conversion/organization rule. Stating a law is possible without discovering it in data; this pass's unselected example establishes feasibility and exposes assumptions, not a prohibition on artistic authorship or a demand for physical mass conservation.

## 7. Unresolved gate and one conditional executable conformance pass

The six task questions resolve as follows: current contribution evidence reaches modeled activity and environmental changes plus registration provenance; the material increment is the retained descriptor, whose generation/extent attribution is still authored. A new law must choose conversion/configuration yield and spatial/vertical identity. Plate-native can express it, but neither that storage nor a field is selected. Extent and repeat are not justified by the current evidence alone. Existing Plate/Z can retain the proposed configuration; live insertion and any intrinsic Character remain separate gates.

Before selecting an endogenous law, name what qualifying activity can change, why it sometimes does not, its extent/neighbor rule, which past conditions participate, whether continued activity can form locally, and its repeat/partition semantics. A true dynamic growth/merge/bond law is allowed; it must be named instead of borrowing observer lineage or scheduler identity. No generic maturity score, attractive topology, natural replay or W2 feature fills this gap.

**Current gate:** no valid empirical discriminator can select a contribution/extent law from these archives alone. An explicit authored commitment or independent formation target is missing. A source-known compiler partition result supplies neither; repacketizing unchanged descriptors tests compiler determinism, not formation/scheduler independence. No experiment is currently recommended merely to restate that result.

To make the next step concrete without silently choosing a law, the following is **one conditional executable specification**, not an approved formation mechanism: if a separate design decision explicitly chooses the restricted event-keyed local-unit contract in §5, test its conversion implementation under duplicate/repacketized delivery. Otherwise stop with `DesignBlocked: contribution/extent/identity law unselected`, before writing a shadow converter or running a fixture. Choosing another law invalidates this specification and requires a replacement, not a multi-candidate implementation. No current user confirmation or production change is requested by this document.
