# Repository guidance

- Read `README.md` before changing the library or demo. For ANT work, use its ANT architecture and next-experiment sections as the project reference.
- Before resuming interrupted work, inspect `git status` and `git diff`; preserve valid partial changes and avoid duplicating them.
- Keep ANT simulation behavior in the framework-independent model. `AntController` coordinates systems, `LocalOccupancy` handles nearby bodies and contact constraints, `PheromoneField` holds shared trail memory, and `AntRenderer` only draws snapshots.
- Work one ANT experiment at a time. The verified SurfaceField/frozen-Plate baseline is `d281d341f5abe68ff98ca4cbf837838c7b7b45a2`, with 191 tests. Read `docs/ant/2026-10-06-surface-plates-handoff.md` and `docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-05-surface-plates/README.md`. Direction-aware traffic is already implemented; do not resume that obsolete handoff.
- The Z-aware stratigraphy/runtime pass is preserved at `1f7011b`; direct-contact sonic baseline `7f8ebf8` supplies the unchanged 3 ms finite force pulse and 75 ms local mechanical recovery, with ×50 Plate damping preserved at `9069a3e`. The founder-refined behavior baseline is frozen at `e38450c`; read `docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-07-founder-effect-refinement/README.md`. The latest completed pass is S0/S1 read-only DynamicSocialGraph, consumed externally after fixed ticks without controller/renderer integration. Read `docs/ant/evidence/2026-10-07-social-graph/README.md` for body-scale proximity semantics, temporal persistence limits, matched ON/OFF evidence and lossless trajectories. Freeze steering, movement, gait, collision, fields, recruitment, Plate birth rules and all audio/material systems. Do not proceed automatically into null-model conclusions, spectral analysis, social feedback, Plate coupling/tuning or runtime work. String/filament and granular/fracture remain design-only extensions.
- Keep `TrailPlateBirth` focused on immutable births; mutable environmental lifetime belongs to `MaterialStratigraphy`. Depth comes only from subsequent overlapping material, never age alone. Ordinary feet excite only exposed top material; deeper material receives energy through reciprocal bounded adjacency. No material or audio feedback enters ANT behavior.
- Freeze AntAgent, LocalOccupancy, GaitController, BodyPoseController, GasterDeposition, PheromoneField and ColonyActivation. Preserve turn 4.5, acceleration 32, brake 37, preferredSpeed ×1.35 cap, trail damping ±2.2, pheromone half-life 15 s, SurfaceField 96×96 /1800 s, real-foot deposition and behavior RNG. Preserve Plate-birth qualification unless fixing a demonstrated bug; technical resource ceilings are separate from qualification.
- Compile infrequent topology outside audio hot loops. Use the existing shared Web Audio engine and one ANT environmental worklet; preserve standalone String/Plate/Sphere and generic DSP. Sleeping skips integration without deleting geometry/history. Expose any technical ceiling and never silently evict material.
- Preserve historical evidence. Material long-run captures include real Chrome motion and final stereo audio at 35% Volume. Sonic passes use controlled one-Plate Chrome AudioWorklet A/B output from identical FOOT_CONTACT streams. Both retain fixed ±1 waveforms and fixed grayscale -100..0 dBFS spectrograms (FFT1024/Hann/hop256/0..8000Hz). The founder-effect pass uses matched natural seeds, fixed-scale maps and silent Chrome motion. Do not normalize clip appearance or manufacture natural milestones.
- Do not claim the procession criterion is met from automated checks alone; inspect the actual visual result.
